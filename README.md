# Mental Smile OS

Arabic-first mental health discovery, support, and ecosystem operating foundation.

Status: Generation 1 Release Candidate  
Architecture: Constitution Frozen  
Execution: Activation Ready With Warnings

## What This Project Is

Mental Smile OS is a governed foundation for a mental health discovery and support ecosystem.

It is designed to help people find supportive content, tools, public resources, providers, centers, and guided spaces through a structured operating model. The project is Arabic-first, with English support, and is built around clarity, safety, ownership, and controlled evolution.

Generation 1 is the first release candidate of that foundation. It brings together the Flutter runtime, Firebase foundation, documentation operating system, governance model, signal federation model, and public showcase documents.

## Why This Matters

Mental health is one of the largest unmet support challenges in the world.

According to the World Health Organization, nearly 1 in 7 people live with a mental disorder. In 2021, that represented about 1.1 billion people globally. WHO also notes that while effective prevention and treatment options exist, most people with mental disorders do not have access to effective care.

The challenge is not only treatment. It is also discovery.

People often struggle to find trustworthy information, understand available options, discover providers, locate support resources, navigate recovery pathways, or even know where to begin.

Mental Smile OS was created to help bridge that discovery and coordination gap through a governed ecosystem built around transparency, accessibility, support, and informed choice.

Source: [World Health Organization (WHO) - Mental Disorders Fact Sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/mental-disorders)

## What This Project Is Not

Mental Smile OS is not a clinic.

It is not a therapy provider.

It is not a diagnosis system.

It is not an emergency service.

It is not a platform for booking, payments, or session control.

The project does not provide clinical advice, diagnosis, treatment promises, or emergency care.

## Technical Foundation

Generation 1 is organized as a federation of governed surfaces and records:

- Zones define independent areas of the ecosystem.
- Gateways control how signals move between zones.
- Signals carry structured activity and discovery information without bypassing boundaries.
- Cards describe governed assets, screens, routes, tools, and records.
- Registries make important system objects visible and traceable.
- Owner Governance controls constitutional changes and release decisions.
- Admin Zero removes legacy all-powerful administrator access from the active model.

The runtime is implemented with Flutter. Firebase is used as part of the active foundation, with release and deployment actions gated separately.

## Safety And Governance

Mental Smile OS is support-oriented and non-diagnostic.

The active governance model is owner-governed, registry-aware, and designed to avoid hidden administrator bypasses. Generation 1 does not introduce booking control, payment control, session control, or clinical decision control.

Public release, deployment, tagging, and production activation require explicit owner approval.
