# EX-117 Fake AI Guarantee Summary

## Fake AI Guarantee Risks

Runtime/provider terminology can create false expectations that the package
cannot satisfy.

## Blocked Guarantee Areas

- AI completion guarantees
- runtime execution guarantees
- adapter execution proof
- backend enforcement proof
- provider causality claims
- provider trust proof
- provider capability proof
- retry/recovery guarantees
- fallback recovery claims
- telemetry/privacy proof
- moderation/safety ownership
- crisis/escalation ownership

## Why Holdback Remains Necessary

Even soft labels like `reported` and `observed` can be misused by consumers as
execution or causality signals once public. The holdback prevents diagnostic
labels from becoming fake runtime guarantees.

## Prevention Doctrine

- labels do not execute
- reports do not prove cause
- observations do not prove state
- references do not prove verification
- fallback terms do not prove recovery
- provider terms do not prove trust
- runtime terms do not prove permission

## Fake Guarantee Verdict

The holdback is justified to prevent runtime/provider labels from becoming
implicit AI guarantees.
