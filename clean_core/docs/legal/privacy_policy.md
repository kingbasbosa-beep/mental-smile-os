# Mental Smile Privacy Policy

Draft only. Requires review by qualified legal counsel before production use.

## Privacy Posture

Mental Smile is designed with a privacy-first and data-minimization posture. The app should collect only information needed to provide wellness support, booking workflows, account access, compliance, and safety foundations.

## App Purpose

Mental Smile supports wellness, awareness, booking, and access to human support resources. It is not a medical diagnosis service, therapy replacement, or emergency service.

## Information Categories

The app may process account information, booking information, provider profile information, consent records, and limited safety/compliance metadata. Future AI safety structures are designed to avoid raw prompt storage, full conversation persistence, diagnosis fields, and unsafe long-term psychological profiling by default.

## AI Status

Runtime AI is not enabled in this phase. Placeholder AI safety architecture exists to support future compliance, auditability, and safety review. If AI features are enabled later, users should be informed that AI may be limited or wrong and is not a doctor, therapist, diagnosis tool, or emergency service.

## Data Minimization

Mental Smile should avoid unnecessary sensitive data, raw crisis content, raw AI conversations, diagnosis labels, medication recommendations, and medical authority fields. Future backend-governed systems should prefer sanitized previews, hashes/placeholders, audit metadata, and server-owned safety decisions.

## Human Support and Crisis Limits

Mental Smile may encourage users to contact trusted people, qualified professionals, or local emergency services. The app cannot guarantee emergency intervention or real-time monitoring.

## Security and Access

The architecture uses a deny-by-default Firestore posture and separates client-writable fields from server-owned governance fields. Future trusted operations should be handled by backend-governed boundaries.

## Review Required

This draft must be reviewed for jurisdiction, platform policy, data processing obligations, retention periods, user rights, minors, and health-adjacent claims before production use.
