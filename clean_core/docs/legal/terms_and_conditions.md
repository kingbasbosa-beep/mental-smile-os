# Mental Smile Terms and Conditions

Draft only. Requires review by qualified legal counsel before production use.

## Purpose

Mental Smile is intended to support wellness, awareness, booking, and access to human support resources. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, not a replacement for therapy, and not an emergency service.

## No Medical or Therapy Replacement

Mental Smile does not provide medical diagnosis, psychiatric diagnosis, treatment plans, medication instructions, or guaranteed clinical outcomes. Any information in the app is general support or service-navigation information only.

Users should seek qualified human professional support for medical, psychological, legal, or emergency needs.

## AI Status

AI safety architecture may exist in the app foundation, but runtime AI support is not enabled in this phase. Future AI features, if enabled, may be limited, may be wrong, and must not be treated as a doctor, therapist, emergency responder, or decision authority.

## Crisis and Emergency Limits

Mental Smile may provide calm guidance encouraging safer steps and local emergency support, but it cannot guarantee rescue, intervention, monitoring, or escalation. If a user or another person is in immediate danger, the user should contact local emergency services or a trusted nearby person.

## User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for providing accurate information, using the app lawfully, and seeking human help when needed. Users must not rely on Mental Smile as the sole source of support during urgent, medical, psychiatric, or safety-critical situations.

## Account and Service Availability

Mental Smile may limit, suspend, or modify app features for safety, maintenance, compliance, or operational reasons. Some features may remain placeholders until reviewed and enabled.

## Data and Privacy

Mental Smile follows a privacy-first and data-minimization posture. Sensitive data should be limited to what is needed for the service. See the Privacy Policy draft for more detail.

## Changes

These terms may change before production release after legal, compliance, and platform review.
