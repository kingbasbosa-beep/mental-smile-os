# Mental Smile AI Safety Disclaimer

Draft only. Requires review by qualified legal counsel before production use.

## Current Status

Runtime AI is intentionally disabled in the current Mental Smile clean-core phase. Existing AI files are safety, governance, and compliance placeholders only.

## Important Limits

Mental Smile AI, if enabled in the future, is not a doctor, not a therapist, not an emergency service, and not a diagnosis or treatment system. It may be limited, incomplete, delayed, or wrong.

## Human Support

Users may still need qualified human support. If a user feels unsafe, at risk, or unable to stay safe, the app should encourage contacting a trusted nearby person, a qualified professional, or local emergency support.

## No Emergency Guarantee

Mental Smile cannot guarantee rescue, escalation, monitoring, or intervention. Crisis support copy is guidance only and must not be presented as emergency response.

## Safety Architecture

The app foundation includes consent contracts, AI governance placeholders, safety-core skeletons, crisis-safe copy, and backend boundary contracts. These structures are intended to support future auditability and safety review before any runtime AI is enabled.
