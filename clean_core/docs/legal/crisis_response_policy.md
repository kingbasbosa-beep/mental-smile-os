# Mental Smile Crisis Response Policy

Draft only. Requires review by qualified legal counsel before production use.

## Purpose

This draft describes the intended crisis-safe posture for Mental Smile. The app is wellness/support/awareness software and is not an emergency service.

## Core Limits

Mental Smile does not diagnose crisis states, does not guarantee intervention, does not dispatch emergency services, and does not replace local emergency support or qualified human care.

## Safe Support Copy

Crisis-safe copy should be calm, short, non-shaming, non-diagnostic, and non-threatening. It may encourage grounding, slowing down, contacting a trusted person, moving to a safer place, or contacting local emergency support when danger is immediate.

## Prohibited Claims

The app must not promise rescue, guarantee safety, claim clinical certainty, provide harmful details, manipulate emotionally, or pressure users with guilt or panic language.

## AI Status

Runtime AI crisis handling is not enabled. Future AI-related crisis workflows must be backend-governed, audited, reviewed, and clearly limited.

## Human Support Direction

When risk or danger is present, Mental Smile should direct users toward trusted human support and local emergency resources rather than presenting itself as the support authority.
