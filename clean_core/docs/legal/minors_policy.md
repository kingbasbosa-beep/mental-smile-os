# Mental Smile Minors Policy

Draft only. Requires review by qualified legal counsel before production use.

## Purpose

This draft sets a foundation for handling minors safely. Final rules depend on jurisdiction, app-store requirements, guardian consent obligations, and the production service model.

## No Diagnosis or Therapy Replacement

Mental Smile is not a medical diagnosis tool, not therapy, and not an emergency service for minors or adults.

## Extra Care for Minors

Features involving minors should use stronger privacy, guardian/parental consent where required, data minimization, and human oversight. The app should avoid unsafe profiling, diagnosis labels, raw crisis content storage, and AI authority over minor safety decisions.

## AI Status

Runtime AI is not enabled in this phase. Future AI features involving minors require additional legal, clinical, platform, and safety review before release.

## Crisis Limits

If a minor or another person is in immediate danger, users should contact local emergency services or a trusted adult nearby. Mental Smile cannot guarantee emergency intervention.
