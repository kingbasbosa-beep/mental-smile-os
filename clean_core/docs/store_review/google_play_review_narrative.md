# Google Play Review Narrative Draft

## App Purpose

Mental Smile is a wellness/support/awareness app focused on support navigation and booking-related workflows. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, or replace professional care.

## AI Status

Runtime AI is intentionally disabled in this phase. Existing AI-related files are safety and governance placeholders only. There are no live OpenAI/Gemini calls, no AI crisis automation, and no backend escalation runtime.

## User Safety

The app uses calm, non-diagnostic support copy. In distress or danger, users are encouraged to contact trusted human support or local emergency services. Mental Smile does not promise emergency intervention.

## Privacy

The architecture favors data minimization and protected server-owned fields. Future AI safety records are designed around sanitized previews, placeholders, policy versions, and audit metadata rather than raw crisis content or full conversations.

## Compliance Readiness

Legal consent, AI safety, crisis copy, and backend boundary foundations are present to support future review before AI runtime features are enabled.

## Claims Avoided

The app does not claim to provide diagnosis, therapy, medication recommendations, emergency response, or guaranteed clinical outcomes.
