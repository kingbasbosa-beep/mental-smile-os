## File Path

`docs/implementation-era/revisions/ACT-01_REVISION_B_SOVEREIGN_KNOWLEDGE_VAULT.md`

## Executive Summary

The historical knowledge of Mental Smile is a sovereign asset because it contains the origin, reasoning, doctrine, prompts, transitions, lessons, decisions, and strategic evolution of the federation. If this knowledge is lost, fragmented, overwritten, or treated as ordinary files, the project may lose the context that explains why it exists and how it should evolve.

This revision adds a new sovereign knowledge asset to the Owner Office: Sovereign Knowledge Vault. The Vault is the protected repository for foundational memory, historical knowledge, strategic evolution, prompt heritage, and long-term project continuity.

Operational Archive and Sovereign Knowledge Vault are related but not identical.

Operational Archive preserves operating records, decisions, incidents, cases, lessons, and department history used for ongoing governance.

Sovereign Knowledge Vault preserves the deeper historical and constitutional memory of the project, including founding assets, master prompts, implementation prompts, wave reports, transition history, strategic decisions, and preserved original evidence.

This document adds governance only. It does not modify runtime, rules, Firebase, Firestore, UI, code, databases, automation, storage systems, backups, or permissions.

- Historical records must not be silently modified.
- Original records remain preserved.
- Analysis may be attached.
- Original evidence remains intact.
- Corrections should be recorded as amendments, not hidden overwrites.
- Superseded records should remain traceable.
- Historical prompts should remain linked to outputs when possible.
- Major knowledge changes require Owner Office review.

## Final Recommendation

Mental Smile should protect project history by treating foundational knowledge, prompts, reports, decisions, lessons, and strategic evolution as a sovereign knowledge asset.

The recommended model is:

- establish Federation Heritage Vault as SKA-001.
- separate Operational Archive from Sovereign Knowledge Vault.
- preserve original records without silent modification.
- classify and preserve prompts that shaped doctrine and implementation.
- define access rights by role.
- maintain backup governance across primary, local, external, and future cloud copies.
- track knowledge risks through a dedicated risk board.
- allow future AI assistance only under Owner authority and historical authenticity rules.

This allows Mental Smile to turn historical knowledge into a long-term strategic asset while preserving authenticity, continuity, and control.
