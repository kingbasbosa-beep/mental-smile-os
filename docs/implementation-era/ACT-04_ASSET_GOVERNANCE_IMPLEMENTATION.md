## Executive Summary

Assets are more important than products because products may change, fail, retire, or transform, while the underlying assets can continue producing value for years. A product may die, but the brand, knowledge, data, templates, reports, processes, prompts, infrastructure accounts, and content behind it may keep creating new products, services, intelligence, and strategic advantage for a decade.

Asset Governance exists to prevent Mental Smile from treating valuable assets as scattered files, tools, accounts, reports, or ideas. It creates ownership, classification, valuation, lifecycle control, reuse logic, risk review, preservation, and commercialization readiness.

This department does not sell, market, decide, or execute product launches. It governs the asset base so Owner Office, Strategic Planning, Finance, Marketing, Technical Studio, and future product teams can make better decisions from a controlled foundation.

This document creates the Asset Governance Department operating structure only. It does not change runtime, rules, Firebase, Firestore, UI, code, databases, automation, or technical implementation.

## Asset Governance Mission

### Purpose

Asset Governance exists to identify, classify, value, preserve, review, and prepare federation assets for reuse, scaling, and future commercialization without losing ownership or strategic control.

### Responsibilities

- Maintain asset registries.
- Classify assets.
- Assign asset ownership.
- Track asset lifecycle.
- Assess asset value.
- Assess asset risks.
- Track asset usage and reuse.
- Assess commercialization readiness.
- Preserve strategic and sovereign assets.
- Feed asset insights to Owner Office and Strategic Planning.

### Authority Boundaries

Asset Governance may classify, review, score, and recommend asset actions.

Asset Governance does not decide final activation, sell products, market services, change infrastructure, modify runtime, approve monetization, or override Owner Office.

### Non-Responsibilities

- sales.
- marketing execution.
- technical implementation.
- financial accounting.
- legal approval.
- product launch.
- Firebase or runtime changes.
- UI or dashboard creation.

### Difference From Other Departments

Owner Office decides final asset, activation, and strategic governance matters.

Strategic Planning plans future use, expansion, and product pathways.

Marketing turns approved assets into external communication and campaigns.

Technical Studio supports technical assets and implementation when activated.

Finance evaluates financial models and revenue impact when activated.

Asset Governance governs what the assets are, who owns them, how valuable they are, where they are in lifecycle, how risky they are, and whether they are ready for reuse or commercialization review.

## Valuation Framework

Asset valuation measures:

### Strategic Value

How important the asset is to federation direction, governance, identity, or future advantage.

### Operational Value

How useful the asset is for current or future operations.

### Commercial Value

How directly or indirectly the asset can support revenue.

### Knowledge Value

How much institutional knowledge, learning, or context the asset preserves.

### Brand Value

How much the asset contributes to trust, recognition, credibility, or positioning.

### Replacement Difficulty

How difficult it would be to recreate, replace, recover, or transfer the asset.

### Risk Exposure

How much damage could occur if the asset is lost, misused, corrupted, or unmanaged.

### Asset Value Score

Asset Value Score is derived from Strategic Value, Operational Value, Commercial Value, Knowledge Value, Brand Value, Replacement Difficulty, and Risk Exposure.

Suggested scoring:

- 0: no meaningful value.
- 1: low value.
- 2: useful.
- 3: important.
- 4: strategic.
- 5: critical.

## Asset Lifecycle Framework

Lifecycle states:

- Proposed.
- Registered.
- Active.
- Governed.
- Commercial Ready.
- Federation Asset.
- Deprecated.
- Archived.
- Retired.

### Proposed

Asset idea exists but is not fully registered.

### Registered

Asset is recorded with owner and classification.

### Active

Asset is currently in use.

### Governed

Asset has owner, lifecycle, risk, review cycle, and archive link.

### Commercial Ready

Asset may support monetization or product/service packaging.

### Federation Asset

Asset supports scaled federation operations and has strong governance.

### Deprecated

Asset should not be expanded and may be replaced.

### Archived

Asset is preserved for history, reference, or traceability.

### Retired

Asset is no longer active.

## Treasury Concept

- All Sovereign Assets.
- All Strategic Assets.
- All Commercial Assets.

Purpose:

- preserve ownership visibility.
- protect strategic value.

Federation Treasury is a governance concept only. It does not create a financial account, database, wallet, UI, or technical system.

## Final Recommendation

Mental Smile can turn assets into scalable products and services by governing assets before commercializing them.

The recommended operating principle is:

- classify before using.
- own before scaling.
- value before monetizing.
- archive before retiring.
- govern before commercializing.
- protect identity before expanding.

Asset Governance should ensure that products and services grow from controlled assets rather than scattered ideas. This allows Mental Smile to create future revenue without losing ownership, fragmenting identity, or weakening the federation's strategic memory.
