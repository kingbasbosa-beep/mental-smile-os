Workforce doctrine enforced:

| Doctrine | Enforced Rule |
|---|---|
| Owner decides but does not execute. | Owner authority is authorization only. |
| Legal interprets but does not deploy. | Legal authority is interpretation only. |
| Technical executes but does not authorize. | Technical authority requires owner/legal authorization. |
| Monitoring observes but does not approve. | Monitoring may verify and escalate only. |
| Compliance audits but does not modify. | Compliance may detect and report only. |
| Archive preserves but does not authorize. | Archive scope remains external to Phase 2. |
| Nobody owns the full lifecycle. | Lifecycle split is enforced by authority cards and permission cards. |
| No critical operation may be completed from one role, identity, or device. | Emergency and constitutional halt cards enforce multi-role escalation. |
