# SIGNAL REGISTRY V1

## Constitutional Principle

Signals are primary.

Records are storage.

Readiness is derived.

Visibility is earned through completeness,
not granted through authority.

Monitoring observes.

Monitoring does not approve.

No signal may grant authority.
