# ARABIC LANGUAGE CONSTITUTION

## Purpose

This constitution governs Arabic generated for Mental Smile. Arabic is an equal
constitutional expression, not a secondary translation of English.

## Voice

Arabic must be:

- clear Modern Standard Arabic;
- warm without becoming sentimental;
- respectful without bureaucratic distance;
- direct without command-and-control language;
- concise enough for interfaces;
- natural to Arabic readers rather than structurally copied from English.

## Formality

- Use respectful neutral address.
- Prefer direct phrases that do not require gender where natural.
- Avoid excessive honorifics, institutional grandeur, and legalistic phrasing
  outside policy contexts.
- Use consistent grammatical person within one surface.
- Do not alternate casually between singular and plural address.

## Person-First Language

- Name the person before a condition or accessibility context when such context
  is necessary.
- Prefer self-described identity and preferences.
- Do not reduce a person to a signal, diagnosis, request, case, or file.
- Do not use permanent labels for temporary needs or states.

Preferred:

- `شخص لديه تفضيلات لإمكانية الوصول`
- `الدعم الذي تفضله`
- `الإشارات التي اخترت مشاركتها`
- `يمكنك استكشاف الموارد`

Avoid:

- `مستخدم معاق`
- `الحالة`
- `المدمن`
- `المريض` when no governed clinical context requires it
- `تم إسنادك`

## Accessibility-First Language

- Describe available formats, controls, and preferences.
- Treat accessibility as a design responsibility, not a personal burden.
- Prefer `إمكانية الوصول`, `تفضيلات التواصل`, and `صيغة ميسرة`.
- Do not use `احتياجات خاصة` as a general constitutional category.
- Do not imply that requesting access support requires approval.

## Recovery-First Language

- Recovery is person-led: `التعافي`, `الأمل`, `النمو`, `الاستمرارية`.
- Use possibility rather than guaranteed outcomes.
- Recognize choice, relationships, strengths, and aspirations.
- Do not frame recovery as obedience, compliance, or institutional ownership.

Preferred:

- `خطوات تدعم اتجاهك نحو التعافي`
- `موارد قد تناسب ما تبحث عنه`
- `اختر ما يدعم رحلتك`

Avoid:

- `يجب أن تلتزم بالخطة`
- `نحن ندير رحلة تعافيك`
- `تم تحديد المسار المناسب لك`

## Non-Stigmatizing Language

- Avoid deficit identities, moral judgment, shame, blame, and permanent
  categorization.
- Do not describe people as failed, broken, weak, dangerous, or non-compliant.
- Describe a current signal, preference, barrier, or safety fact only when known.

## Non-Diagnostic Language

- Signals express interests, preferences, declarations, or observed events.
- Never convert signal patterns into diagnoses.
- Use `قد`, `ربما`, or an explicit evidence boundary when uncertainty exists.
- Do not use `أنت تعاني من` or `أنت بحاجة إلى` unless a qualified, governed
  source and context explicitly support the statement.
- Generated copy must not infer treatment, severity, or clinical identity.

## Agency

Prefer verbs that preserve choice:

- `استكشف`
- `اختر`
- `اعرض`
- `شارك`
- `تواصل`
- `احفظ`
- `تابع عندما تكون مستعدًا`

Avoid authority verbs in person-facing copy:

- `وافق`
- `ارفض`
- `عيّن`
- `فعّل الحساب`
- `تحكم`
- `أدر الحالة`

Safety instructions may be direct only when the urgency is real and declared.

## Readiness and Visibility

- Use `الجاهزية` for derived completeness.
- Use `جاهز`, `غير مكتمل`, `مسودة`, or `محظور لأسباب تتعلق بالسلامة` when
  those states are factual.
- Do not use `مقبول`, `مرفوض`, or `بانتظار الموافقة` for visibility.
- Visibility is `الظهور` or `قابلية الظهور`, not human activation.

## Monitoring

- Monitoring is `الرصد` or `الملاحظة` according to the registered meaning.
- It observes signals and states.
- It does not own, assign, approve, or manage people.
- Avoid translating observatory language into an operations or control center.

## Forbidden Arabic Patterns

The following patterns block generation when they carry active doctrine:

- `تمت الموافقة على`
- `بانتظار الموافقة`
- `تم رفض`
- `تم تعيين`
- `مسؤول الحالة`
- `مالك الحالة`
- `مالك قائمة الانتظار`
- `غرفة التحكم`
- `لوحة الإدارة`
- `التحكم في الجلسة`
- `دورة الحجز`
- `موافقة الدفع`
- `إدارة رحلة التعافي`
- `تفعيل مقدم الخدمة`
- `الحالات المفتوحة` when people or conversations are treated as owned work

Historical quotations must be clearly marked as historical and are never
reusable UI patterns.

## Preferred Arabic Patterns

| Purpose | Preferred Pattern |
|---|---|
| Navigation | `انتقل إلى {destination}` or the destination name alone |
| Discovery | `استكشف {resources}` |
| Optional support | `يمكنك طلب دعم فني` |
| Signal sharing | `شارك الإشارات التي تختارها` |
| Readiness | `الجاهزية: {state}` |
| Missing information | `أكمل {item} لتحسين الجاهزية` |
| Recommendation | `قد يناسبك {resource}` |
| Empty state | `لا توجد {items} حتى الآن` |
| Recoverable error | `تعذر {action}. حاول مرة أخرى.` |
| Safety | `إذا كان هناك خطر فوري، تواصل مع خدمات الطوارئ المحلية.` |

## Arabic Quality Checks

Before acceptance, verify:

1. correct UTF-8 Arabic without mojibake;
2. natural Arabic order rather than literal English structure;
3. stable person and level of formality;
4. correct gender-neutral construction where feasible;
5. no stigma, diagnosis, or authority drift;
6. equivalent urgency and choice to English;
7. approved registry terms are used consistently;
8. punctuation and placeholders do not break bidirectional display.

## Arabic Parity Rule

Arabic may be shorter or structurally different from English, but it must not:

- add stronger commands;
- remove uncertainty;
- add institutional authority;
- weaken a safety warning;
- intensify stigma;
- promise a better outcome;
- change who owns the action.
