## Strongest Registries

1. **CLIENT_REGISTRY:** Eight active person-led concepts and clear boundaries around identity, aspirations, choice, privacy, and signals.
2. **MARKETPLACE_REGISTRY:** Seven active terms distinguish discovery, resources, capabilities, destinations, and contact without booking ownership.
3. **MONITORING_REGISTRY:** Seven active observational terms clearly separate visibility and safety observation from control.
4. **RECOVERY_REGISTRY:** Seven active terms ground recovery in person-led hope, growth, continuity, and support.

## Weakest Registries

1. **BRANDING_REGISTRY:** Only one active term; product voice, naming conventions, tagline governance, and brand-expression rules remain undefined.
2. **POLICY_REGISTRY:** It is extensive but heavily transitional or forbidden: only 5 of 22 terms are active. This reflects unresolved vocabulary migration rather than weak policy importance.
3. **PROVIDER_REGISTRY and CENTER_REGISTRY:** Core identity and capability terms exist, but room, document, availability, media, and declaration vocabulary still includes future or contaminated boundaries.

## Missing Concepts

- A governed **AUTH vocabulary owner** for sign-in, account identity, access, verification, and credential recovery.
- A dedicated **SAFETY vocabulary owner** for crisis guidance, abuse, fraud, legal intervention, and safety escalation.
- Owner Room and Personal Space naming families beyond generic governance/client terms.
- Consent, data rights, retention, erasure, and signal-sharing language.
- Arabic style governance: grammatical gender, plurality, formality, action verbs, and accessibility-first phrasing.
- AI output constraints for diagnosis avoidance, deficit avoidance, and person-first Arabic generation.
- Explicit distinctions among declaration, signal, record, resource, capability, and recommendation in user-facing contexts.

## Constitutional Freeze Blockers

1. **106 keys** have no proven direct non-generated reference and require lifecycle adjudication.
2. **7,003 hardcoded candidates** require qualification because the scan includes real copy and false-positive code fragments.
3. **771 old-doctrine findings** show booking, approval, assignment, session, administration, and operational vocabulary still exists in the localization landscape.
4. **22 duplicate-meaning groups** lack an approved canonical-term decision.
5. Locale parity is incomplete: `statusCenterFollowUp` is missing from English.
6. No automated doctrine-compliance gate currently resolves copy against this registry.
7. AUTH and SAFETY concepts have consumers in the audited landscape but no dedicated registry in the requested architecture.
8. Transitional terms do not yet have a consumer-by-consumer retirement ledger.

## Governance Decision

**The registry foundation is established, but constitutional localization freeze is not yet ready.** The language system now has a source of truth; implementation evidence still requires classification and migration under that governance.
