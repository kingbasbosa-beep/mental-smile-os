## Problem

## Finding

## Evidence

- Phase 11 requested Organizations Directory, Partnership Request Flow, and Partnership Registry.

## Impact

Without a foundation, Mental Smile cannot collect partnership intent or prepare a governed accessibility organization directory.

## Status
