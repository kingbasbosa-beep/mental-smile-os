# Card Governance Guide V1

Status: ACTIVE_BASELINE_GUIDE
Classification: MISSING_CREATE
Authority: Owner Governance
Runtime effect: none

## Doctrine

Cards are governance units, not documentation.

A card records why an object exists, who owns it, who consumes it, what it depends on, whether it is active, whether it is legacy, and whether it is archived.

## Card Scope

Cards may govern:

- routes
- assets
- collections
- guides
- signals
- ownership
- archive records
- tools
- findings

## Rules

- A file can exist without authority.
- A card gives governed identity.
- A card does not create runtime permission.
- A card does not override Owner approval.
- Legacy items require cards before purification.
