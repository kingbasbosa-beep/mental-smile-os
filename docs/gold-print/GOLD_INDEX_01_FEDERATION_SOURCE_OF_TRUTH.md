## First Implementation Sequence

### ACT-01 - Owner Office

Reason: sovereign decisions, no-return doctrine, and review authority must exist before activation.

### ACT-02 - Archive

Reason: institutional memory must capture decisions, outcomes, reports, and lessons before expansion.

### ACT-03 - Strategic Planning

Reason: activation, capacity, product, and expansion planning need structured review before departments scale.

### ACT-04 - Asset Governance

Reason: products, tools, content, data, templates, domains, and AI assets must be owned before they multiply.

### ACT-05 - Strategic Intelligence

Reason: recommendations require evidence, archive memory, and Owner review before they can guide future decisions.

This order prevents action from outrunning governance.
