# ROOM MODEL REFERENCE

## Purpose

Rooms are governed product spaces. Each room has a purpose, audience, assets,
signals, language, and future evolution path.

## Room Map

| Room | Purpose | Audience | Primary Signals | Language Doctrine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Client Room | Personal identity, aspirations, tools, saved resources, and self-expression | Clients | Desired identity, hope, support style, growth, discovery, privacy | Person-first and non-diagnostic |
| Provider Room | Provider declarations, capabilities, communication, accessibility, and readiness | Providers | Identity, capability, document, accessibility, communication | Declaration and readiness |
| Center Room | Center declarations, location, services, accessibility, media, and readiness | Centers | Location, capability, pricing, media, document | Capability and visibility readiness |
| Monitoring Room | Observe readiness, signals, safety, drift, and freshness | Observers, owner | Readiness, missing signals, visibility, safety | Observation only |
| Owner Room | Strategy, ratification, registry evolution, and federation direction | Owner | Governance readiness, product health, risk, opportunity | Stewardship and constitutional decision |

## Client Room

Assets:

- background;
- TV screen zone;
- tool board;
- notebook;
- profile monitor;
- exit cup;
- navigation arrows;
- empty tool slot;
- future themes.

Future evolution:

- signal communication board;
- magnet-based tools and resources;
- aspiration cards;
- accessibility preferences;
- room themes;
- capsule-compatible personal spaces.

## Provider Room

Assets:

- provider identity visuals;
- capability visuals;
- registration and declaration components;
- future room backgrounds.

Future evolution:

- self-managed declaration completeness;
- capability cards;
- accessibility and communication signals;
- readiness visibility without approval.

## Center Room

Assets:

- center identity visuals;
- media placeholders;
- service and capability visuals;
- future center room components.

Future evolution:

- center capability maps;
- residential and accessibility signals;
- media-ready discovery;
- readiness without operational lifecycle ownership.

## Monitoring Room

Assets:

- observatory visuals;
- signal visuals;
- safety visuals;
- readiness displays.

Future evolution:

- drift detection;
- registry health;
- readiness observability;
- safety-only intervention visibility;
- no assignment or approval.

## Owner Room

Assets:

- strategic memory surfaces;
- governance dashboards;
- registry health summaries;
- owner decision packages.

Future evolution:

- federation roadmap control;
- capsule release ratification;
- registry version governance;
- strategic learning loops.

## Room Admission Rule

A new room requires:

- constitutional purpose;
- audience;
- card registry entry;
- asset registry entry;
- language ownership;
- signal ownership;
- allowed actions;
- forbidden actions;
- monitoring boundary;
- future capsule inheritance path.
