## Executive Summary

Human Impact Office is not a normal support department. It is a Mission Protection Department inside the Mental Smile Federation. Its purpose is to protect the human meaning of the project while the federation grows operationally, commercially, technically, and institutionally.

Human Impact Office exists because growth can easily become product-centered, revenue-centered, or operations-centered. Mental Smile must preserve human dignity, accessibility, protection, prevention, continuity, family support, and ethical responsibility as active operating concerns, not slogans.

This department is classified as an Owner Protected Department, Human Impact Department, Non-Revenue First, and Mission Driven. It reports directly to Owner Office and does not create new execution authority. It coordinates programs, gaps, partnerships, content needs, and impact intelligence to keep the federation aligned with its human mission.

This document creates the Human Impact Office operating structure only. It does not change runtime, rules, Firebase, Firestore, UI, code, databases, automation, or technical implementation.

## Doctrine

## Human Impact First Doctrine

Human Impact First means Mental Smile must preserve human dignity and mission responsibility while building services, products, content, partnerships, and growth systems.

The doctrine covers:

- Human Dignity.
- Accessibility.
- Protection.
- Prevention.
- Continuity.
- Family Support.
- Ethical Responsibility.

## What We Do

- identify human-impact gaps.
- support accessibility planning.
- support family awareness and education.
- support children protection and prevention awareness.
- support recovery continuity signals and programs.
- coordinate human-impact content needs with Education Services.
- identify partnerships that support accessibility, families, children, recovery, and community needs.
- produce impact reports and gap reports.
- keep Owner Office aware of mission-sensitive risks.

## What We Do Not Do

- provide clinical diagnosis.
- provide treatment.
- replace professional care.
- make emergency decisions.
- govern providers, centers, or partners.
- override Owner Office.
- change platform rules or runtime.
- create UI or technical systems.
- use AI as human-impact decision authority.

## Mission

### Purpose

Human Impact Office exists to protect the human mission layer of Mental Smile through accessibility, family support, children protection and prevention, recovery continuity, and human impact intelligence.

### Responsibilities

- Human Impact First doctrine stewardship.
- accessibility program coordination.
- family support program coordination.
- children protection and prevention program coordination.
- recovery continuity program coordination.
- human impact intelligence.
- human-impact gap detection.
- human-impact partnership identification.
- human-impact content strategy coordination.
- archive linkage for major human-impact decisions and lessons.

### Authority Boundaries

Human Impact Office may identify needs, coordinate programs, recommend protections, produce reports, and escalate mission-sensitive issues to Owner Office.

Human Impact Office does not decide sovereign matters, provide clinical services, execute technical changes, approve legal commitments, or operate as a crisis service.

### Non-Responsibilities

- final Owner decisions.
- clinical diagnosis or treatment.
- emergency response.
- legal approval.
- finance approval.
- marketing execution.
- technical implementation.
- Firebase or runtime changes.
- UI creation.

## Final Recommendation

Human Impact Office can preserve the human mission of Mental Smile while the federation grows commercially by keeping accessibility, family support, children protection, recovery continuity, and ethical responsibility directly visible to Owner Office.

The recommended operating principle is:

- mission before revenue.
- dignity before growth.
- accessibility before expansion.
- prevention before reaction.
- continuity before abandonment.
- family support before isolation.
- Owner protection before mission drift.

This allows Mental Smile to grow as a federation without losing the human reason it exists.
