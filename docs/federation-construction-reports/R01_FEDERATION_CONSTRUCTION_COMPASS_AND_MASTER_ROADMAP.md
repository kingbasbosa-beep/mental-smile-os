# R01 - FEDERATION_CONSTRUCTION_COMPASS_AND_MASTER_ROADMAP

## File Path

## Executive Summary

This report establishes the Federation Construction Compass and Master Roadmap for Mental Smile. It defines the strategic direction for building a federated institutional system where App, Web, Archive, Strategic Intelligence, Owner Room, Control Room, and Departments each retain a clear sovereign role.

The compass is not a technical implementation plan. It is an architectural governance document that protects Mental Smile from role confusion, premature automation, operational overreach, and admin-panel drift. Its main purpose is to align future construction waves around one doctrine:

Departments execute. Archive remembers. Strategic Intelligence analyzes. Owner decides. AI recommends. Humans decide.

The master roadmap organizes construction into five strategic layers:

1. Sovereign foundations: Web intake, Archive, Owner Room, and Strategic Intelligence as institutional primitives.
2. Operating surfaces: App as marketplace runtime and provider/center operating surface.
3. Observability surfaces: Control Room as monitoring, support, escalation, and operational awareness.
4. Activation structures: Products, departments, services, gates, and readiness criteria.
5. Governance convergence: final alignment through the unified execution master plan.

The roadmap confirms that Federation Construction must not convert the Web into an Admin Panel, must not convert the Control Room into God Mode, and must not move sovereign decision authority away from the Owner Room.

## Recommendation

Adopt this compass as the governing orientation for the Federation Construction wave. All future reports, product activation plans, department plans, and execution gates should be checked against the role boundaries defined here before any implementation planning begins.

Recommended sequencing:

1. Use R01 as the strategic compass.
2. Use R02 to realign policy, rules, and compliance doctrine.
3. Use R03 to define activation gates before operational rollout.
4. Use R04 to map products, departments, and services to activation readiness.
5. Use R05 only after R01-R04 are complete, as the final convergence report.
