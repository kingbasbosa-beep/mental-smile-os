# R03 - FEDERATION_ACTIVATION_GATES

## File Path

`docs/federation-construction-reports/R03_FEDERATION_ACTIVATION_GATES.md`

## Executive Summary

This report defines Federation Activation Gates for Mental Smile. Activation Gates are strategic readiness checkpoints that prevent premature rollout, role confusion, and accidental technical implementation before institutional doctrine is clear.

The gates are not code gates, deployment gates, Firestore gates, or UI release gates. They are architectural decision checkpoints that determine whether a product, department, service, surface, or governance capability is mature enough to move from blueprint into planned execution.

The Federation Activation Gates are:

1. Doctrine Gate: confirms that the capability respects the Mental Smile institutional doctrine.
2. Ownership Gate: confirms who decides, who executes, who remembers, who analyzes, and who monitors.
3. Boundary Gate: confirms that Web, App, Owner Room, Control Room, Archive, and Strategic Intelligence are not being collapsed into each other.
4. Human Authority Gate: confirms that AI remains advisory and humans remain decision owners.
5. Memory Gate: confirms what must be archived and how institutional memory should be preserved.
6. Escalation Gate: confirms what belongs to Control Room as observability, support, and escalation.
7. Activation Readiness Gate: confirms whether the capability is ready for future implementation planning.

These gates create a disciplined path from strategy to execution without turning the report itself into implementation.

## Assumptions

- Activation is a strategic status, not a technical deployment.
- Passing a gate does not create UI, Firebase, runtime behavior, or rules.
- A capability can be strategically approved while still requiring future implementation planning.
- Owner Room is the sovereign decision system.
- Control Room monitors and escalates but does not become God Mode.
- Archive preserves institutional memory.
- Strategic Intelligence supports institutional learning but does not decide.
- Departments execute only after doctrine, ownership, and readiness are clear.

## Recommendation

Use the following activation states across future Federation Construction documents:

- Drafted: concept exists but has not passed doctrine alignment.
- Aligned: concept respects institutional doctrine.
- Scoped: ownership and boundaries are clear.
- Gate-Ready: concept is ready for formal activation review.
- Activated for Planning: concept may enter future planning, still without implementation.
- Blocked: concept violates doctrine, ownership, authority, or boundary rules.

No product, department, service, or governance surface should move into implementation planning unless it passes the Doctrine Gate, Ownership Gate, Boundary Gate, and Human Authority Gate.
