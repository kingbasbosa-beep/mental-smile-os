# R04 - FEDERATION_PRODUCTS_DEPARTMENTS_AND_SERVICES_ACTIVATION_PLAN

## File Path

`docs/federation-construction-reports/R04_FEDERATION_PRODUCTS_DEPARTMENTS_AND_SERVICES_ACTIVATION_PLAN.md`

## Executive Summary

This report defines the strategic activation plan for Federation Products, Departments, and Services. It translates the Federation doctrine into a planning structure for deciding which institutional capabilities can be prepared for future execution.

This report does not activate runtime behavior. It does not create services, collections, routes, dashboards, UI, Firebase integrations, or Firestore rules. It only defines how products, departments, and services should be classified and prepared within the Mental Smile federation.

The activation plan separates three institutional categories:

- Products: value surfaces and offerings that Mental Smile may present, operate, or evolve.
- Departments: human or operational units that execute defined responsibilities.
- Services: structured operational capabilities that support product and department workflows.

Each category must remain aligned with the core doctrine:

- Products do not become sovereign decision authorities.
- Departments execute but do not rewrite institutional doctrine.
- Services support operations but do not override human ownership.
- Archive records institutional memory.
- Strategic Intelligence learns and recommends.
- Owner Room decides.
- Control Room monitors, supports, and escalates.

## Assumptions

- Demo Productization Blueprint is completed and can inform future product classification.
- Existing constitutional doctrine is authoritative.
- Products, departments, and services require activation planning before implementation.
- Booking, commission, and payout logic are out of scope.
- Future Finance/Legal remains future-facing and is not activated by this report.
- Control Room is operational monitoring, not sovereign command.
- Owner Room remains the sovereign decision system.
- AI recommendations may support activation planning but cannot approve activation alone.

## Recommendation

Adopt a three-track activation structure:

1. Product Activation Track
   - Define product intent.
   - Define audience and operating context.
   - Define institutional owner.
   - Define memory requirements.
   - Define monitoring and escalation needs.
   - Confirm no sovereign authority is embedded into the product itself.

2. Department Activation Track
   - Define department mandate.
   - Define execution scope.
   - Define escalation path.
   - Define Archive obligations.
   - Define Strategic Intelligence feedback loop.
   - Confirm department does not become policy owner unless delegated by Owner Room.

3. Service Activation Track
   - Define service purpose.
   - Define service users.
   - Define operational limits.
   - Define human decision checkpoints.
   - Define observability needs.
   - Confirm service does not automate sovereign decisions.

Future activation plans should produce a readiness profile for each product, department, and service before any implementation planning begins.
