# MENTAL SMILE FEDERATION PRODUCT BLUEPRINT V1

## Status

Strategic blueprint. No code, Firebase, route, asset, localization, or migration
effect.

## 1. Product Identity

Mental Smile is a governed federation for recovery, discovery, accessibility,
knowledge, support, providers, centers, resources, signals, and future AI
assistance. It is not merely a Flutter application. The application is one
expression of a larger living ecosystem.

Mental Smile is:

- a city of governed rooms, districts, registries, cards, and signals;
- a federation where people, providers, centers, resources, and knowledge can
  be discovered through declared meaning;
- a constitutional system where language, assets, signals, and cards are
  governed before they scale;
- a recovery-aware ecosystem that supports choice without owning the person's
  journey;
- an AI-ready product system where future assistants inherit doctrine instead
  of inventing it.

Mental Smile is not:

- a booking platform;
- an admin control room;
- a case ownership system;
- a payment, payout, accounting, or session lifecycle;
- a system that approves identity or manually grants human visibility;
- a recovery owner;
- a collection of uncontrolled features.

## Era Transition

### Booking Platform Era

The system once centered on operational lifecycle language: booking, sessions,
inboxes, approval, assignment, administration, and archive. That era treated
many surfaces as managed work.

### Marketplace Era

The next shape shifted toward discovery: providers, centers, services, contact,
public visibility, resources, and user choice. This moved the product away from
platform-owned transactions but still carried legacy vocabulary and structures.

### Constitutional Federation Era

The current doctrine is federation:

```text
Cards
Signals
Readiness
Declarations
Resources
Safety
Recommendations
```

Visibility is derived from readiness. Language is governed. Assets are
registered. Signals are primary. AI is constrained by registries. Rooms and
districts become product architecture, not decorative metaphors.

## 2. Federation Topology

The federation is built from layered authorities:

```text
Constitution Layer
Registry Layer
Signal Layer
Card Layer
Asset Layer
Language Layer
Room Layer
District Layer
Capsule Layer
AI Layer
Monitoring Layer
Owner Layer
```

### Constitution Layer

The Constitution Layer defines what may exist. It declares forbidden doctrine,
active concepts, governance boundaries, and no-return rules.

### Registry Layer

The Registry Layer is the source of truth for terms, assets, signals, cards,
capabilities, and future domains. It prevents uncontrolled growth.

### Signal Layer

The Signal Layer captures declared interests, preferences, readiness, safety,
support needs, resource interactions, and future matching inputs. Signals do
not diagnose and do not grant authority.

### Card Layer

The Card Layer gives product objects a constitutional identity. Cards describe
systems, signals, tools, content, resources, categories, safety, archives,
magnet objects, aspirations, and pathways.

### Asset Layer

The Asset Layer governs visual material: room components, icons, backgrounds,
branding, placeholders, future concepts, and legacy imports.

### Language Layer

The Language Layer governs meaning, vocabulary, Arabic and English parity,
generation rules, forbidden terms, transitional language, and AI copy.

### Room Layer

Rooms are personal or role-based product spaces: Client Room, Provider Room,
Center Room, Monitoring Room, and Owner Room. A room has assets, signals,
language, cards, and allowed actions.

### District Layer

Districts are city-scale public or semi-public domains: Providers, Centers,
Knowledge, Recovery, Accessibility, Tools, Apps, Family, Partnership, and future
districts.

### Capsule Layer

Capsules are governed clones or packaged product units that inherit doctrine,
assets, language, signals, cards, reports, prompts, documentation, and backups.

### AI Layer

The AI Layer contains future assistants for assets, localization, signals,
themes, archives, recommendations, and factories. AI composes within doctrine.
It does not legislate.

### Monitoring Layer

The Monitoring Layer observes readiness, signals, safety, freshness, registry
coverage, drift, and system health. It does not approve or assign.

### Owner Layer

The Owner Layer ratifies strategy, doctrine, freeze decisions, registry
evolution, capsule release, and high-risk governance choices. It does not run
daily operations.

## 3. City Model

Mental Smile City is the public ecosystem surface where people discover
resources, providers, centers, knowledge, tools, accessibility paths, family
support, and partnerships.

### Providers District

Purpose: discover provider declarations, capabilities, accessibility, and
communication preferences.

Audience: clients, families, providers, observers.

Signals: provider identity, capability, availability, accessibility,
communication, document, and readiness signals.

Cards: provider category cards, capability cards, provider cards, readiness
cards.

Future direction: signal-aware provider discovery without assignment.

### Centers District

Purpose: discover center declarations, locations, services, residential
capabilities, accessibility, pricing signals, and media.

Audience: clients, families, centers, observers.

Signals: center identity, location, capability, accessibility, pricing, media,
document, and readiness signals.

Cards: center category cards, capability cards, center cards, readiness cards.

Future direction: center comparison through declared signals and user choice.

### Knowledge District

Purpose: provide educational, recovery, accessibility, and policy knowledge.

Audience: public visitors, clients, families, providers, centers.

Signals: interest, reading, saved content, support category, learning intent.

Cards: content cards, knowledge cards, pathway cards, resource cards.

Future direction: knowledge becomes magnet-ready and personalized through
signals chosen by the person.

### Recovery District

Purpose: support hope, continuity, growth, strengths, aspirations, and recovery
resources.

Audience: clients, families, support observers, knowledge contributors.

Signals: aspiration, motivation, strength, continuity, support style, hope.

Cards: client aspiration cards, recovery resource cards, pathway cards.

Future direction: recovery pathways built from signals without diagnosis.

### Accessibility District

Purpose: make communication, navigation, content, discovery, and rooms accessible
through preferences and formats.

Audience: everyone, especially people with accessibility preferences.

Signals: communication format, access preference, language preference, assistive
mode, environment need.

Cards: accessibility cards, tool cards, format cards, preference cards.

Future direction: accessibility becomes a first-class matching dimension.

### Tools District

Purpose: provide user-facing tools that help reflection, discovery, support,
knowledge, and room personalization.

Audience: clients, providers, centers, owners, observers.

Signals: tool usage, saved outputs, preference, topic interest.

Cards: tool cards, signal cards, pathway cards.

Future direction: tools become signal-aware and recommend resources without
controlling outcomes.

### Apps District

Purpose: host future app-like capsules and specialized product experiences.

Audience: clients, providers, centers, partners, owners.

Signals: capsule use, feature interest, access preference, resource discovery.

Cards: app cards, capsule cards, tool cards.

Future direction: governed product expansion through capsule inheritance.

### Family District

Purpose: support family relationships, belonging, education, communication, and
continuity resources.

Audience: family members, clients, caregivers, knowledge readers.

Signals: relationship, belonging, motivation, communication preference, support
area.

Cards: relationship cards, content cards, resource cards, support cards.

Future direction: family support that respects consent and person-led recovery.

### Partnership District

Purpose: describe collaboration opportunities, resource partnerships, public
channels, and institutional relationships.

Audience: partners, centers, providers, public entities, owner.

Signals: partnership interest, capability, geography, public resource type.

Cards: partnership cards, resource cards, policy cards.

Future direction: federation-scale collaboration without centralized ownership.

### Future Districts

Purpose: reserve governed expansion areas before runtime is created.

Audience: owner, registry stewards, future capsule builders.

Signals: concept readiness, registry coverage, asset readiness, language
readiness.

Cards: future concept cards, capsule cards, governance cards.

Future direction: districts are admitted only when their doctrine, cards,
assets, language, and signals are ready.

## 4. Room Model

### Client Room

Purpose: a personal space for identity, aspirations, hope, preferred support,
tools, saved resources, signals, and self-expression.

Assets: room background, TV zone, tool board, notebook, profile monitor, exit
cup, navigation arrows, empty tool slots, future themes.

Signals: desired identity, aspiration, hope, belonging, support style, growth,
discovery, accessibility, privacy.

Language: person-first, recovery-first, choice-preserving, non-diagnostic.

Future evolution: a signal-rich room where tools, content, and resources appear
through magnet matching.

### Provider Room

Purpose: a space for provider declarations, capabilities, accessibility,
visibility readiness, communication preferences, and future provider tools.

Assets: provider identity visuals, capability visuals, room components, future
backgrounds.

Signals: identity, capability, document, accessibility, communication,
availability, learning, safety.

Language: declaration and readiness language, never approval or assignment.

Future evolution: provider self-governance through declarations and signal
completeness.

### Center Room

Purpose: a space for center identity, location, capability declarations,
accessibility, pricing signals, media, documents, and visibility readiness.

Assets: center room background, registration visuals, media placeholders,
capability cards.

Signals: identity, location, service, accessibility, pricing, media, document,
safety.

Language: declaration and capability language, not approval, booking, or
accommodation lifecycle ownership.

Future evolution: center discovery and comparison through declared signals.

### Monitoring Room

Purpose: observe signals, readiness, safety, freshness, drift, and registry
health.

Assets: observatory visuals, signal visuals, safety visuals, monitoring room
components.

Signals: readiness, missing signals, visibility, freshness, safety, drift.

Language: observation only. No control, approval, assignment, or ownership.

Future evolution: federation observability and governance intelligence.

### Owner Room

Purpose: constitutional direction, strategy, registry ratification, freeze
decisions, capsule release, and high-risk governance decisions.

Assets: owner room components, observability summaries, strategic memory
surfaces.

Signals: governance readiness, registry status, product health, risk,
opportunity, roadmap state.

Language: governance, stewardship, boundaries, decisions, and ratification.

Future evolution: strategic operating room for federation direction, not daily
operations.

## 5. Signal Economy

Signals exist because records alone cannot express living intention. A record is
storage. A signal is meaning with direction.

Signals flow from:

```text
People
Providers
Centers
Resources
Tools
Rooms
Safety Events
Registries
```

Signals become:

```text
Readiness
Visibility
Recommendations
Observability
Cards
Resource Magnetism
Future Intelligence
```

Signal lifecycle:

1. declared or observed;
2. stored with source and context;
3. classified by registry;
4. aggregated where allowed;
5. consumed by readiness or recommendations;
6. observed by monitoring;
7. retired, refreshed, or superseded.

Signal ownership stays with its source and governing registry. Monitoring may
observe signals. AI may interpret patterns within constraints. No signal grants
authority over a person.

Future signal intelligence will connect fingerprints, resources, tools,
providers, centers, pathways, and learning without diagnosis or assignment.

## 6. Card Economy

Cards are constitutional product objects.

### Signal Cards

Represent declared or observed signals with boundaries and consumers.

### Asset Cards

Represent visual assets, ownership, lifecycle, destination, and migration
readiness.

### Term Cards

Represent governed language, meaning, lifecycle, allowed contexts, forbidden
contexts, and replacement terms.

### Capability Cards

Represent provider or center capabilities in a discoverable and comparable
form.

### Registry Cards

Represent systems, registries, rooms, observatories, and foundations.

### Future Card Doctrine

No active product object should remain cardless. No card should legitimize
contaminated legacy. Cards become the passport for build eligibility.

## 7. Asset Governance

The Asset Constitution defines visual ownership. The Asset Registry prevents
duplicate ownership, orphan images, uncontrolled placeholders, and unknown
visuals.

Asset lifecycle:

```text
ACTIVE
DORMANT
LEGACY
ORPHAN
DEAD
UNCLASSIFIED
```

The Dormant Vault holds future-use assets with known purpose. It is not a dump.

The Theme System allows room variants such as Pharaonic, Modern, Minimal,
Recovery, Nature, and Accessibility rooms, but only through compatible controls,
boards, TV zones, notebooks, profile screens, and registered dependencies.

The future Asset Factory creates governed visuals from cards and registries. The
future Asset Assistant helps classify, generate, validate, and prepare assets
without moving them outside doctrine.

## 8. Language Governance

The Language Constitution defines vocabulary, tone, Arabic and English parity,
forbidden doctrine, and AI generation rules.

The Language Registry owns terms. Term Cards define meaning. The Language Engine
generates constitutional language, not arbitrary translations. The Language
Operating System explains how language is born, evolves, freezes, and passes to
future capsules.

AI Language Rules require registry ownership first, reject forbidden doctrine,
use replacements automatically, maintain Arabic and English parity, and never
invent terminology.

The future Language Assistant will validate copy, propose governed language,
detect drift, and prepare migrations without changing doctrine by itself.

## 9. Capsule Architecture

A Capsule is a governed package of product capability that can be cloned,
localized, themed, deployed, backed up, or evolved without breaking the central
constitution.

What can be cloned:

- code modules;
- assets;
- localization;
- signal schemas;
- card sets;
- reports;
- prompts;
- documentation;
- backups;
- theme variants;
- public experiences;
- selected city districts or room templates.

What remains centralized:

- constitutional doctrine;
- term ownership;
- forbidden vocabulary;
- signal doctrine;
- card doctrine;
- asset lifecycle rules;
- safety boundaries;
- owner ratification;
- registry versioning;
- AI generation rules.

Capsule inheritance model:

```text
Central Constitution
-> Registry Version
-> Capsule Manifest
-> Capsule Assets
-> Capsule Language
-> Capsule Signals
-> Capsule Cards
-> Capsule Runtime
-> Capsule Reports
-> Capsule Backup
```

A capsule may specialize expression. It may not fork doctrine.

## 10. AI Ecosystem

### Asset Assistant

Classifies assets, proposes registry destinations, prepares generation prompts,
and checks visual ownership.

### Localization Assistant

Validates language, detects forbidden doctrine, proposes bilingual copy, and
maintains parity.

### Theme Assistant

Builds room theme definitions from registered controls, assets, and compatible
visual zones.

### Signal Assistant

Maps source events into signal fingerprints, suggests signal cards, and prepares
magnet matching inputs.

### Archive Assistant

Preserves historical documentation and separates it from active runtime and
active doctrine.

### Future Factory Systems

resource maps, and recommendation foundations from approved registries.

review, old control room, and old archive doctrine. It now has foundations for
signals, cards, assets, language, S rooms, readiness, and product governance.

destination, asset ownership, language ownership, and doctrine status.

signal-aware and magnet-ready.

assets, language, and signal doctrine.

Mental Smile can package specialized experiences for new audiences, regions,
themes, partners, and future products without losing constitutional alignment.
