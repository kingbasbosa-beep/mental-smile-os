# OPS-03 - EDUCATION_SERVICES_IMPLEMENTATION

## File Path

`docs/operations-era/OPS-03_EDUCATION_SERVICES_IMPLEMENTATION.md`

## Executive Summary

Education Services is not only a content library. It is the federation's Knowledge Production Engine. It creates, governs, reviews, packages, updates, and commercializes educational knowledge while protecting quality, reliability, accessibility, and intellectual property.

Inside Mental Smile, Education Services supports the library, provider enablement, center enablement, awareness content, internal learning, and future educational products.

Externally, Education Services can produce guides, courses, programs, workshops, training packages, certification tracks, awareness kits, and licensed educational assets.

The department must avoid becoming a mass-content factory. More content does not mean better knowledge. Education Services must operate through ownership, review cycles, source governance, accessibility standards, content lifecycle, licensing rules, and archive linkage.

This document creates the Education Services operating structure only. It does not change runtime, rules, Firebase, Firestore, UI, code, databases, automation, or technical implementation.

## Education Services Mission

### Purpose

Education Services exists to produce and govern educational knowledge assets that support trust, learning, provider readiness, center readiness, awareness, and future educational revenue.

### Responsibilities

- content governance.
- content production.
- content review.
- educational program design.
- course services.
- library management.
- knowledge packaging.
- research and publications.
- content partnerships.
- accessibility services.
- content ownership and licensing tracking.
- archive linkage for major content assets.

### Authority Boundaries

Education Services may create, review, classify, package, and recommend educational content and products.

Education Services does not make clinical decisions, publish medical claims without review, decide final product activation, own sovereign policy, change runtime, create UI, or bypass Owner Office governance.

### Non-Responsibilities

- final Owner decisions.
- medical diagnosis.
- treatment claims.
- legal approval.
- finance approval.
- marketing execution.
- technical implementation.
- Firebase or runtime changes.
- UI creation.

## Organization Structure

## Content Governance

Purpose: define rules for content ownership, rights, review, lifecycle, and visibility.

Inputs: content assets, source references, ownership records, licensing status.

Outputs: governance decisions, content status, review rules.

Dependencies: K01, DOC Registry, Archive.

Reports: Content Governance Report.

## Content Production

Purpose: produce educational materials, guides, scripts, articles, and learning assets.

Inputs: content priorities, provider/center needs, research references, strategic gaps.

Outputs: draft content, educational assets, content packages.

Dependencies: Content Governance, Strategic Planning, Marketing Studio.

Reports: Content Production Report.

## Content Review

Purpose: review quality, accuracy, tone, source integrity, accessibility, and publication readiness.

Inputs: draft content, source material, review criteria, content cards.

Outputs: approved content, revision requests, rejected content, review notes.

Dependencies: Content Review Registry, Archive, future clinical/legal review when needed.

Reports: Content Review Report.

## Educational Programs

Purpose: design structured programs and learning paths.

Inputs: learning goals, audience needs, content assets, provider/center enablement needs.

Outputs: program outlines, learning paths, program readiness notes.

Dependencies: Strategic Planning, Content Asset Registry.

Reports: Program Design Report.

## Course Services

Purpose: design courses and course packages.

Inputs: course goals, content assets, audience segment, learning outcomes.

Outputs: course outlines, modules, course readiness briefs.

Dependencies: Educational Programs, Content Review.

Reports: Course Services Report.

## Library Management

Purpose: organize content inside the library as governed knowledge assets.

Inputs: content cards, categories, usage signals, archive links.

Outputs: library maps, category health notes, content gap reports.

Dependencies: Content Asset Framework, Library Intelligence.

Reports: Library Management Report.

## Knowledge Packaging

Purpose: convert content into guides, kits, courses, training packages, and future products.

Inputs: approved content, demand signals, strategic value, revenue mapping.

Outputs: packaged knowledge assets, product candidates, training bundles.

Dependencies: Asset Governance, Marketing Studio, Technical Studio.

Reports: Knowledge Packaging Review.

## Research & Publications

Purpose: manage research references, educational publications, and public-safe knowledge outputs.

Inputs: research content, external references, strategic topics, archive memory.

Outputs: publication drafts, reference summaries, research-based educational material.

Dependencies: Content Governance, Archive, Strategic Intelligence.

Reports: Research & Publications Report.

## Content Partnerships

Purpose: manage partner content, licensed content, and external educational collaboration.

Inputs: partner proposals, licensing terms, source records, content needs.

Outputs: partner content records, licensing notes, collaboration briefs.

Dependencies: Owner Office, future Legal, Asset Governance.

Reports: Content Partnership Review.

## Accessibility Services

Purpose: ensure educational content can serve diverse audiences.

Inputs: content assets, audience needs, accessibility standards.

Outputs: accessibility notes, adaptation recommendations, accessible content formats.

Dependencies: Content Review, Technical Studio when implementation is authorized.

Reports: Accessibility Review.

## Accessibility Framework

## Deaf Users

Content should support captions, text clarity, and visual alternatives where relevant.

## Blind Users

Content should support screen-reader-friendly structure, clear text, and non-visual alternatives where future implementation allows.

## Low Literacy Users

Content should support plain language, short structure, and guided formats.

## Children

Content should use age-appropriate language and require careful review.

## Families

Content should support practical explanation and responsible guidance.

## Special Needs Audiences

## Final Recommendation

Education Services can become a knowledge and revenue engine by governing educational content before scaling it.

The recommended operating principle is:

- quality before quantity.
- ownership before publication.
- rights before reuse.
- review before release.
- accessibility before expansion.
- archive before retirement.
- packaging before commercialization.

This allows Mental Smile to create trusted educational products and services without sacrificing reliability, intellectual property, or long-term knowledge control.
