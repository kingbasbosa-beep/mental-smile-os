## Executive Summary

Marketing Studio is not only an advertising department. It is a federation growth and revenue engine that connects brand trust, visibility, content, provider promotion, center promotion, campaign services, customer acquisition, partnership marketing, and future paid marketing products.

Inside Mental Smile, Marketing Studio supports federation promotion, brand consistency, audience development, product visibility, educational promotion, and growth campaigns.

Externally, Marketing Studio can produce services for providers, centers, partners, and future clients through campaign services, promotion packages, consultation, advertising operations, subscription services, and premium visibility support.

The department must grow revenue without damaging trust, identity, content quality, or mental health positioning. Marketing Studio must operate under Owner Office governance, brand governance, asset governance, archive memory, and future policy review.

This document creates the Marketing Studio operating structure only. It does not change runtime, rules, Firebase, Firestore, UI, code, databases, automation, or technical implementation.

## Marketing Studio Mission

### Purpose

Marketing Studio exists to grow Mental Smile visibility, support federation products, create trusted promotional systems, and develop internal and external marketing services that may become revenue streams.

### Responsibilities

- campaign management.
- content marketing.
- brand marketing.
- advertising operations.
- promotion design.
- customer acquisition.
- provider promotion services.
- center promotion services.
- partnership marketing.
- community growth.
- marketing asset governance.
- campaign reporting and archive linkage.

### Authority Boundaries

Marketing Studio may plan, propose, execute approved campaigns, produce marketing materials, manage campaign reports, and recommend promotion opportunities.

Marketing Studio does not decide sovereign brand doctrine, approve sensitive campaigns alone, change platform runtime, create UI placements, alter rules, sell unapproved products, override content governance, or make medical claims.

### Non-Responsibilities

- final Owner decisions.
- clinical or therapeutic claims.
- legal approval.
- finance approval.
- technical implementation.
- Firebase or runtime changes.
- UI creation.
- independent brand creation.
- unapproved advertising placement.

## Advertising Framework

## Federation Advertising Framework

Advertising is allowed only when trust, visibility, content quality, brand governance, and review requirements are respected.

## Approved Ad Locations

Rules: must be approved by Owner Office and future policy review.

Limits: no misleading claims, no medical guarantees, no excessive placement density.

Review Requirements: brand review, content review, risk review.

## Banner Areas

Rules: banners must support clear user value and avoid clutter.

Limits: placement quantity and frequency must be controlled.

Review Requirements: campaign review and future UI authorization before implementation.

## Library Promotions

Rules: promotions must align with content quality and education standards.

Limits: promotional content must not weaken knowledge trust.

Review Requirements: content governance review.

## Provider Promotions

Rules: provider promotions must be truthful, clear, and profile-readiness aligned.

Limits: no medical claims, no guaranteed outcomes, no misleading superiority.

Review Requirements: provider readiness review and content/brand review.

## Center Promotions

Rules: center promotions must reflect actual center services and readiness.

Limits: no exaggerated claims or unverified capability.

Review Requirements: center readiness review and content/brand review.

## Sponsored Placements

Rules: sponsorship must be clearly identified.

Limits: must not compromise trust or search/discovery fairness.

Review Requirements: Owner Office and future policy/legal review.

## Educational Promotions

Rules: must be educationally responsible and clearly positioned.

Limits: no treatment replacement claims.

Review Requirements: education and content review.

## Final Recommendation

Marketing Studio can become a growth and revenue source for the Mental Smile Federation by building trust-first marketing services.

The recommended operating principle is:

- trust before conversion.
- brand governance before promotion.
- content quality before advertising.
- provider/center readiness before sponsored visibility.
- consent before direct messaging.
- archive every important campaign decision.
- measure performance without letting metrics override identity.

This allows Marketing Studio to generate internal growth and external revenue without damaging trust, identity, or content quality.
