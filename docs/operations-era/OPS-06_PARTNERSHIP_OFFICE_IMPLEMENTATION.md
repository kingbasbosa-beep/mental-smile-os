## File Path

## Executive Summary

Partnership Office is not only public relations. It is a Strategic Expansion Department responsible for discovering, evaluating, developing, coordinating, and tracking partnerships that can expand the Mental Smile Federation's reach, credibility, services, knowledge, education, technology, community presence, and future revenue.

Partnerships are not just relationships. They can become channels for education, community support, institutional credibility, sponsorships, co-marketing, technology enablement, healthcare collaboration, media reach, and market expansion.

Partnership Office must expand federation opportunities without becoming a contracts office, legal authority, sales authority, or sovereign decision layer. It identifies and develops partnership opportunities, but Owner Office retains final approval, future Legal handles legal review when activated, and Archive preserves partnership history.

This document creates the Partnership Office operating structure only. It does not change runtime, rules, Firebase, Firestore, UI, code, databases, automation, or technical implementation.

## Partnership Office Mission

### Purpose

Partnership Office exists to grow federation influence and opportunity through governed institutional relationships, strategic alliances, community partnerships, educational partnerships, corporate partnerships, sponsorships, and future collaboration channels.

### Responsibilities

- partnership discovery.
- partnership evaluation.
- partnership development.
- partnership coordination.
- strategic alliance mapping.
- community partnership support.
- educational partnership support.
- institutional partnership support.
- corporate partnership support.
- government relations support.
- sponsor relations.
- partnership reporting and archive linkage.

### Authority Boundaries

Partnership Office may identify, evaluate, coordinate, and recommend partnership opportunities.

Partnership Office does not sign contracts, make sovereign decisions, approve legal commitments, approve financial commitments, govern partners, override Owner Office, or activate partnerships without review.

### Non-Responsibilities

- final Owner decisions.
- legal contract approval.
- financial approval.
- sovereign policy.
- partner internal management.
- technical implementation.
- marketing execution without Marketing Studio coordination.
- Firebase or runtime changes.
- UI creation.

## Final Recommendation

Partnership Office can expand federation influence and opportunity by developing relationships without becoming a contracting authority or sovereign decision layer.

The recommended operating principle is:

- discover before proposing.
- evaluate before negotiating.
- align before approving.
- archive before forgetting.
- Owner review before commitment.
- future Legal and Finance review before binding obligations.

This allows Mental Smile to grow institutional reach, sponsorships, education opportunities, community presence, and strategic alliances while protecting federation governance and authority boundaries.
