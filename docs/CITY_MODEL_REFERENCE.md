# CITY MODEL REFERENCE

## Purpose

Mental Smile City is the federation-scale product model. Districts organize
discovery, knowledge, resources, tools, providers, centers, accessibility,
family support, partnerships, and future domains.

## City Principles

1. A district is a governed ecosystem domain.
2. A district contains cards, signals, language, assets, and future capsules.
3. A district enables discovery and choice.
4. A district does not own people, journeys, bookings, or cases.
5. A district may be public, personal, role-based, or future-reserved.

## City Runtime Boundary

The city may contain discovery, saved destinations, contact requests, resources,
content, public profiles, recommendations, and safety guidance.

The city must not contain booking ownership, assignment, approval, queue
ownership, operational archive, or platform-owned recovery.

## City Growth Rule

A new district requires:

- name and purpose;
- audience;
- owner registry;
- card set;
- signal model;
- language ownership;
- asset ownership;
- monitoring model;
- no-return boundary;
- future capsule compatibility.
