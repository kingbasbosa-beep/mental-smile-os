# EXEC-D - GOVERNANCE_HARDENING_PACKAGE

## File Path

`docs/execution-wave/EXEC-D_GOVERNANCE_HARDENING_PACKAGE.md`

## Executive Summary

EXEC-D strengthens federation governance before real activation begins. Governance Hardening must happen before department activation because activation without governance creates authority drift, brand drift, policy drift, asset chaos, and legacy resurrection.

The Mental Smile Federation now has foundation registries, Digital Twin templates, and a manual Knowledge Operations Layer. Before departments begin operating at scale, the federation needs a hardened governance layer for brand, assets, health, policy alignment, and legacy decisions.

This package transforms Governance, Brand, Assets, Policies, Health, and Legacy Decisions into a unified manual operating structure that can later scale without changing its logic.

This package documents governance only. It does not create Firebase, Firestore, rules changes, runtime changes, UI, automation, databases, dashboards, or implementation.

## Federated Brand Registry

The Federated Brand Registry is the official manual registry for visual identity governance.

Scope:

- Parent Brand.
- Department Variants.
- Visual Governance.
- Report Governance.
- Archive Governance.
- Print Governance.

Required governance areas:

### Primary Logo

Mental Smile remains the parent brand identity. A single parent logo model is the default unless future Owner-approved hybrid variants are authorized.

### Department Logo Variants

Department variants may exist only as controlled lockups or markers under the Mental Smile parent brand. They must not create independent brands.

### Color System

Department colors are governance markers for clarity, reports, archive markers, twins, and future dashboards. They do not create separate visual sovereignty.

### Icon System

Icons may be used in future documents or dashboards as clarity markers. They must remain consistent with the parent brand and not imply separate authority.

### Report Cover Rules

Reports should show Mental Smile parent identity, report wave, report ID, department or system marker, date, and status.

### Document Identity Rules

Documents should use consistent naming, wave classification, owner, status, archive linkage, and review cycle.

### Archive Marker Rules

Archive markers should indicate status, visibility, historical value, strategic value, and linkage to decisions, incidents, cases, or lessons.

## Asset Lifecycle Model

Asset lifecycle states:

- Proposed.
- Planned.
- Active.
- Governed.
- Federation Ready.
- Deprecated.
- Retired.
- Archived.

### Proposed

Asset idea exists but is not approved.

### Planned

Asset is approved for planning but not operational.

### Active

Asset is in use.

### Governed

Asset has owner, lifecycle, review cycle, risk level, and archive linkage.

### Federation Ready

Asset can support scaled federation operations.

### Deprecated

Asset should not be expanded and may be replaced.

### Retired

Asset is no longer active.

### Archived

Asset is preserved for history, reference, or traceability.

## Federation Health Model

Federation Health Governance integrates:

- Department Health.
- Content Health.
- Archive Health.
- Knowledge Health.
- Control Room Health.
- Growth Readiness.

### Department Health

Measures department identity, capacity, reporting, governance, assets, and strategic alignment.

### Content Health

Measures freshness, quality, coverage, demand, priority, and governance.

### Archive Health

Measures completeness, traceability, linkage, resolution, strategic value, and historical value.

### Knowledge Health

Measures content, archive, evidence, reporting, and trigger health.

### Control Room Health

Measures observability, signal quality, reporting, escalation, response, and coverage.

### Growth Readiness

Measures whether growth can be absorbed through capacity, content, operations, governance, brand, and policy readiness.

### Federation Health Score

Federation Health Score should be derived from the six health areas above. It is a review indicator, not an automatic activation command.

## Health Review Cycle

### Weekly

Inputs: critical signals, open loops, active incidents, urgent risks.

Review Owners: relevant department owner, Control Room, Owner Office when needed.

Outputs: urgent health notes, escalation items, open loop updates.

Archive Destination: Incident Registry, Case Registry, or Archive Backlog.

### Monthly

Inputs: department reports, content health, archive backlog, evidence packs, trigger registry.

Review Owners: Strategic Intelligence and Owner Office.

Outputs: monthly health report, risk notes, recommendations.

Archive Destination: Strategic Archive and Historical Registry.

### Quarterly

Inputs: capacity reviews, product readiness, market analysis, department maturity, policy alignment.

Review Owners: Owner Office, Strategic Intelligence, relevant department owners.

Outputs: quarterly federation health review, readiness decisions, delay or expansion recommendations.

Archive Destination: Strategic Archive and Decision Registry.

### Annual

Inputs: yearly outcomes, health history, no-return doctrine, legacy risks, brand consistency, asset lifecycle.

Review Owners: Owner Office.

Outputs: annual governance review, constitutional review candidates, next-year direction.

Archive Destination: Historical Registry and Strategic Archive.

## Legacy Governance Map

Legacy areas:

- Booking Era.
- Admin-Centric Era.
- Legacy Accounting Logic.
- Legacy Terminology.
- Legacy Routes.
- Legacy Collections.
- Legacy Workflows.

Classification model:

- Keep.
- Freeze.
- Archive.
- Retire.
- Replace.

### Booking Era

Keep: useful marketplace learnings.

Freeze: booking-first identity.

Archive: old booking assumptions.

Retire: booking as central doctrine.

Replace: governed marketplace and provider/center operating surface.

### Admin-Centric Era

Keep: useful observability lessons.

Freeze: admin expansion mindset.

Archive: legacy admin decisions.

Retire: God Mode assumptions.

Replace: Control Room observability and escalation doctrine.

### Legacy Accounting Logic

Keep: relevant finance lessons.

Freeze: premature finance system assumptions.

Archive: old accounting decisions.

Retire: accounting-first workflows if misaligned.

Replace: future finance governance.

### Legacy Terminology

Keep: terms that remain accurate.

Freeze: ambiguous or outdated terms.

Archive: historical language.

Retire: booking-first and admin-centric language.

Replace: federation operating language.

### Legacy Routes

Keep: useful user pathways when aligned.

Freeze: routes that imply incorrect authority.

Archive: old routing assumptions.

Retire: misaligned legacy routes in future authorized implementation.

Replace: future sovereign and operating route architecture.

### Legacy Collections

Keep: concepts that remain valid.

Freeze: unclear or obsolete data assumptions.

Archive: historical collection logic.

Retire: misaligned legacy collection models in future authorized implementation.

Replace: future registry-aligned data models if authorized.

### Legacy Workflows

Keep: workflows that still support operations.

Freeze: workflows with unclear ownership.

Archive: historical process assumptions.

Retire: workflows that conflict with federation doctrine.

Replace: governed department and registry workflows.

## Governance Health Model

Governance Health should be measured through:

### Policy Coverage

Measures whether important areas have policies.

### Policy Compliance

Measures whether current practices align with policy.

### Asset Coverage

Measures whether material assets are registered and owned.

### Brand Consistency

Measures whether departments and reports follow federated brand governance.

### Legacy Risk Coverage

Measures whether legacy risks are identified, classified, and reviewed.

### Review Discipline

Measures whether governance items are reviewed on schedule.

### Governance Health Score

Governance Health Score should be derived from Policy Coverage, Policy Compliance, Asset Coverage, Brand Consistency, Legacy Risk Coverage, and Review Discipline.

Suggested scoring:

- 0: unmanaged.
- 1: fragmented.
- 2: partially documented.
- 3: operational.
- 4: governed.
- 5: federation ready.

## Manual Governance Workflow

Manual workflow:

1. Register brand, asset, policy, legacy, or health item in the relevant registry.
2. Assign owner, status, review cycle, and archive link.
3. Classify lifecycle or risk state.
4. Review manually on weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annual cycle.
5. Route strategic issues to Strategic Intelligence.
6. Route sovereign decisions to Owner Review.
7. Archive decisions, outcomes, lessons, and superseded items.
8. Update Governance Health Score.

No Firebase is required.

No databases are required.

No dashboards are required.

No automation is required.

## Final Recommendation

EXEC-D helps Mental Smile prevent Authority Drift, Brand Drift, Policy Drift, Asset Chaos, and Legacy Resurrection before department activation begins.

The recommended approach is:

- Harden brand governance before public expansion.
- Register assets before they multiply.
- Align policies before departments operate.
- Track federation health before scale pressure.
- Classify legacy before old assumptions return.
- Archive all major governance decisions.
- Keep Owner Review as the final authority for material governance changes.

This package prepares the federation to activate departments from a stable governance base rather than from scattered assumptions.
