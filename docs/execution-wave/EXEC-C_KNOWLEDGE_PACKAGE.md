# EXEC-C - KNOWLEDGE_PACKAGE

## File Path

`docs/execution-wave/EXEC-C_KNOWLEDGE_PACKAGE.md`

## Executive Summary

EXEC-C converts knowledge, archive memory, signals, evidence, and reports into a manual Knowledge Operations Layer that supports better decisions. It does not create Firebase, UI, dashboards, databases, automation, runtime behavior, or rules.

The purpose of this package is to make knowledge operational. Content should not remain a passive library. Archive should not remain storage. Reports should not be disconnected documents. Signals should not disappear after they are noticed. Evidence should not remain scattered. Recommendations should not be made without traceable support.

This package creates the manual operating layer that moves Mental Smile from scattered knowledge to decision-support knowledge. It defines the Content Priority Map, Content Gap Registry, Archive Backlog System, Strategic Trigger Registry, Evidence Registry, Reporting Calendar, Knowledge Flow Architecture, Open Loop Detection, Knowledge Health Model, Visual Knowledge Governance, Manual Operations Workflow, and Future Migration Readiness.

## Content Priority Map

Content should be classified into:

- Critical.
- Strategic.
- High.
- Medium.
- Low.
- Archive Candidate.

### Critical

Activation Rules: required for trust, safety, core operations, policy clarity, or essential user/provider/center understanding.

Review Frequency: monthly or after major change.

Update Priority: immediate.

Department Ownership: responsible operating department plus Owner review when governance-sensitive.

### Strategic

Activation Rules: supports product readiness, market positioning, education, institutional learning, or strategic decisions.

Review Frequency: quarterly.

Update Priority: high.

Department Ownership: Strategic Intelligence, Education, Marketing Studio, Archive, or relevant department.

### High

Activation Rules: supports active workflows, repeated support needs, provider/center enablement, or operational clarity.

Review Frequency: quarterly or when signals indicate decay.

Update Priority: high to medium.

Department Ownership: relevant operating department.

### Medium

Activation Rules: useful but not required for immediate activation.

Review Frequency: semiannual.

Update Priority: normal.

Department Ownership: relevant department or Content Studio when activated.

### Low

Activation Rules: optional, background, or future-facing.

Review Frequency: annual or when demand rises.

Update Priority: low.

Department Ownership: relevant department.

### Archive Candidate

Activation Rules: content is outdated, superseded, historically useful, or no longer operationally active.

Review Frequency: before archival or retirement.

Update Priority: archive or retire decision.

Department Ownership: Archive plus original content owner.

## Content Gap Registry

- Department.
- Category.
- Description.
- Evidence.
- Demand Level.
- Strategic Impact.
- Recommended Action.
- Status.
- Review Date.

Statuses:

- Open.
- Under Review.
- Accepted.
- Deferred.
- In Progress.
- Closed.
- Archived.

The registry should prevent content gaps from remaining informal complaints or scattered notes.

## Archive Backlog System

The Archive Backlog System records archive work that must be completed manually.

Backlog categories:

- Reports To Archive.
- Decisions To Archive.
- Incidents Missing Lessons.
- Open Historical Records.
- Unlinked Archive Items.

### Priority Rules

Critical priority:

- missing Owner decisions.
- unresolved incidents.
- open loops.
- no-return doctrine records.
- strategically important reports.

High priority:

- major department changes.
- activation decisions.
- product decisions.
- risk decisions.

Medium priority:

- historical records with moderate relevance.
- content evolution records.
- department reports.

Low priority:

- reference-only history.
- superseded low-impact records.

### Review Cycles

- Weekly: open loops and critical missing records.
- Monthly: decision and incident backlog.
- Quarterly: historical records and unlinked items.

### Closure Rules

An archive backlog item closes only when:

- required fields are complete.
- owner is defined.
- archive link exists.
- status is updated.
- lesson or outcome is captured when required.

## Evidence Registry

The Evidence Registry is the official record of evidence used for recommendations, trigger reviews, and strategic decisions.

Each Evidence Pack includes:

- Evidence ID.
- Source Type.
- Source Name.
- Collection Date.
- Reliability Score.
- Freshness Score.
- Strategic Relevance.
- Departments Affected.
- Related Recommendations.
- Archive Links.

Evidence sources:

- Firebase Analytics.
- Control Room Reports.
- Department Reports.
- Archive Records.
- Market Research.
- Competitor Analysis.
- Customer Suggestions.
- Provider Suggestions.
- Center Suggestions.
- Support Intelligence.
- Complaint Intelligence.

Evidence governance rules:

- Firebase Analytics is a signal source, not a standalone decision source.
- Suggestions are evidence inputs, not proof.
- Complaints and support trends should be linked to Archive when repeated or strategic.
- Evidence should be scored before recommendations depend on it.
- Evidence Packs should link to recommendations and Archive outcomes.

Suggested scoring:

- 0: unreliable or missing.
- 1: anecdotal.
- 2: partial.
- 3: usable.
- 4: strong.
- 5: validated.

## Reporting Calendar

### Weekly

Purpose: track near-term operational signals, support issues, open loops, urgent content gaps, and active triggers.

Inputs: Control Room reports, support intelligence, complaint intelligence, open archive items, department notes.

Outputs: weekly signal review, open loop list, urgent actions for Owner review if needed.

Owner: relevant department owner or Owner Office during founder-operated phase.

Archive Destination: Archive Core Registry for important cases and decisions.

Strategic Consumption: Strategic Intelligence uses repeated weekly patterns.

### Monthly

Purpose: review content health, archive backlog, evidence packs, trigger registry, department signals, and recommendations.

Inputs: Content Gap Registry, Archive Backlog, Evidence Registry, Trigger Registry, department reports.

Outputs: monthly knowledge operations report and recommendation candidates.

Owner: Strategic Intelligence with Owner Office review.

Archive Destination: Strategic Archive and Historical Registry.

Strategic Consumption: used for recommendation drafting and capacity review.

### Quarterly

Purpose: review strategic direction, readiness, capacity, product signals, department maturity, and market changes.

Inputs: monthly reports, market research, competitor analysis, archive lessons, evidence packs, department health.

Outputs: quarterly strategic review, product readiness notes, expansion/delay recommendations.

Owner: Strategic Intelligence and Owner Office.

Archive Destination: Strategic Archive.

Strategic Consumption: informs Owner planning and next activation decisions.

### Annual

Purpose: review federation learning, knowledge health, archive health, strategy, no-return decisions, and major roadmap changes.

Inputs: quarterly reviews, decision outcomes, archive history, content health, federation health, market trends.

Outputs: annual federation learning report and constitutional review candidates if needed.

Owner: Owner Office.

Archive Destination: Historical Registry and Strategic Archive.

Strategic Consumption: informs next-year strategy.

### Extraordinary

Purpose: respond to major incident, urgent opportunity, severe risk, market shock, governance drift, or Owner request.

Inputs: emergency signals, incident reports, evidence packs, risk registry entries, department escalation.

Outputs: extraordinary review package and Owner decision record.

Owner: Owner Office.

Archive Destination: Incident Registry, Decision Registry, Strategic Archive.

Strategic Consumption: used for urgent recommendations and post-event learning.

## Open Loop Detection

Open loop rules detect:

- Missing Evidence.
- Missing Outcome.
- Missing Review.
- Missing Lesson.
- Missing Archive Link.
- Missing Owner.

An item should be flagged as open loop when:

- a decision has no outcome.
- an incident has no lesson.
- a recommendation has no Owner decision.
- evidence has no source or archive link.
- content gap has no review date.
- trigger has no status.
- archive item has no owner.

Open loops should be reviewed weekly when critical and monthly when non-critical.

## Knowledge Health Model

Knowledge Health is derived from:

### Content Health

Measures freshness, quality, coverage, demand, priority, and governance.

### Archive Health

Measures completeness, traceability, linkage, resolution, strategic value, and historical value.

### Evidence Health

Measures reliability, freshness, relevance, traceability, and diversity.

### Reporting Health

Measures reporting discipline, timeliness, completeness, usefulness, and archive linkage.

### Trigger Health

Measures whether triggers have evidence, thresholds, recommendations, Owner review, and archive links.

### Knowledge Health Score

Knowledge Health Score should be derived from Content Health, Archive Health, Evidence Health, Reporting Health, and Trigger Health.

Suggested scoring:

- 0: unmanaged.
- 1: fragmented.
- 2: partially structured.
- 3: operational.
- 4: governed.
- 5: federation ready.

## Manual Operations Workflow

Manual workflow:

1. Record content gaps, signals, evidence, triggers, and archive backlog items in the relevant registry.
2. Assign ID, owner, status, review date, and archive linkage.
3. Score evidence reliability and freshness.
4. Classify content priority.
5. Review open loops weekly or monthly.
6. Produce reporting outputs on the defined calendar.
7. Strategic Intelligence drafts recommendations when evidence supports review.
8. Owner reviews recommendations.
9. Decisions and outcomes return to Archive.
10. Knowledge Health Score is updated on review cycles.

No Firebase is required.

No databases are required.

No dashboards are required.

No automation is required.

## Final Recommendation

EXEC-C helps Mental Smile prevent knowledge loss by turning content gaps, archive backlogs, evidence, triggers, and reports into governed manual operations.

It turns knowledge into actionable decision support by ensuring every important signal can become evidence, every evidence pack can support recommendations, every recommendation can go to Owner review, and every decision can return to Archive.

The recommended next step is to operate this package manually before building dashboards or databases. Manual discipline should prove the governance model before automation is considered.
