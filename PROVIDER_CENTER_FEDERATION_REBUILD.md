# Provider & Center Federation Rebuild

## Scope

Bundle C creates provider and center federation foundations.

It does not create UI, widgets, screens, Firebase writes, Firestore
collections, runtime flows, session logic, booking logic, payment logic,
notifications, or automation.

## Provider Doctrine

Provider Room is based on:

- identity
- visibility
- trust
- contact
- reputation
- guidance

It is not based on session management.

## Center Doctrine

Center Room is based on:

- identity
- visibility
- trust
- contact
- reputation
- guidance

It is not an operational control surface.

## Contact Doctrine

Official action:

- Contact
- تواصل

The new federation foundation rejects lifecycle wording for modern provider and
center surfaces.

## Public Reputation

Reputation uses:

- General Rating
- Collapsible Comments
- Reputation Summary

It does not use session rating.

## Tool Meter

Tool Meter alignment connects provider and center visibility to usage,
adoption, trust, and impact language without runtime calculations.

## Guidance

Guidance alignment prepares:

- Easy Reading
- Screen Reader
- Audio Labels
- Learn More

## Archive

Provider and center archive profiles are reference-only and do not archive
runtime data in this wave.

## Readiness

Provider and center readiness alignment connects the future rebuild to
readiness, roadmap, and separation markers.
