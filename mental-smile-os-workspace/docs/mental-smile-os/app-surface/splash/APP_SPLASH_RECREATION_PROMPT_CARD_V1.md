## Prompt Text

Create a minimal calm Arabic-first mental wellbeing app splash screen for Mental Smile. Use a soft warm visual identity, clean centered brand mark placeholder, Arabic welcome line, short non-clinical support phrase, simple rounded continue button, optional Arabic/English language toggle placeholder, and subtle loading/status text. The screen should feel supportive, clear, and uncluttered.

## Style Rules

- Arabic-first layout.
- Warm soft background.
- Minimal brand mark.
- One primary button.
- Comfortable spacing.
- Calm non-clinical tone.

## Negative Prompt / Avoid Rules

- No medical imagery.
- No clinical claims.
- No diagnosis language.
- No booking language.
- No provider links.
- No ads.
- No crowded UI.
- No copied legacy splash background.

Future App Surface Splash visual recreation and refinement.
