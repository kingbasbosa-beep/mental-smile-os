# DNA Vault Signal Model V1

Status: ACTIVE_CONSTITUTION
Signal authority: DNA Vault

## 1. Signal Boundary

DNA Vault signals are constitutional expansion signals. They do not create domains automatically. They identify validated constitutional states that may permit or block construction.

## 2. Allowed Signals

| Signal ID | Signal Name | Meaning | Allowed Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DNA_SIGNAL_001 | NEXT_REQUIRED_DOMAIN | DNA Vault has identified the next domain required by constitutional dependency order. | Identification only unless separately authorized. |
| DNA_SIGNAL_002 | CONSTITUTIONAL_GAP_DETECTED | A required doctrine, custody structure, dependency, or authority is missing. | Blocks expansion until resolved. |
| DNA_SIGNAL_003 | CONSTITUTIONAL_ALIGNMENT_REQUIRED | Existing source material or proposed construction must be aligned before use. | Blocks activation and requires alignment work. |
| DNA_SIGNAL_004 | CONSTITUTIONAL_EXPANSION_APPROVED | A proposed expansion passes sequencing, dependencies, and prerequisites. | Permits creation of the approved target only. |
| DNA_SIGNAL_005 | CONSTITUTIONAL_EXPANSION_REJECTED | A proposed expansion violates DNA Vault rules or lacks prerequisites. | Forbids creation until conditions change. |

## 3. Emission Requirements

Every DNA Vault signal must include:

- Signal ID.
- Signal name.
- Trigger condition.
- Constitutional justification.
- Dependency result.
- Action boundary.

## 4. Signal Prohibitions

DNA Vault signals may not:

- Carry operational instructions.
- Create more than the identified constitutional target.
- Smuggle future planning into the active OS.
- Ratify a legacy room by name without replacement analysis.
- Authorize Strategic Planning, Sovereign Tools, Technical Operations, Legal, Archive, Monitoring, Research, or Operations by assumption.

## 5. Signal Authority

Only DNA Vault may emit DNA signals. External documents may request validation, but they cannot emit binding DNA signals.
