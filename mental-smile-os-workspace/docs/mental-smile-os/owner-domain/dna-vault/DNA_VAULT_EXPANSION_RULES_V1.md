# DNA Vault Expansion Rules V1

Status: ACTIVE_CONSTITUTION
Authority: DNA Vault

## 1. Expansion Principle

Expansion is forbidden by default. A new domain may exist only when DNA Vault proves it is constitutionally required, dependency-ready, minimal, non-duplicative, and aligned.

## 2. Absolute Prohibitions

The following are forbidden until DNA Vault explicitly approves them:

- Manual future planning.
- Manual domain ordering.
- Manual department creation.
- Manual expansion decisions.
- Strategic Planning.
- Sovereign Tools.
- Technical Operations.
- Legal domains.
- Monitoring domains.
- Research domains.
- Archive domains.
- Operational domains.
- Any future domain.

## 3. Expansion Approval Gate

A proposed expansion must pass:

1. Custody relevance check.
2. Constitutional gap check.
3. Dependency check.
4. Legacy non-cloning check.
5. Minimal structure check.
6. Removal and alignment check.
7. DNA signal emission.

## 4. Expansion Signals

- Approval requires DNA_SIGNAL_004.
- Rejection requires DNA_SIGNAL_005.
- Missing prerequisite requires DNA_SIGNAL_002.
- Alignment need requires DNA_SIGNAL_003.
- Next-domain identification requires DNA_SIGNAL_001.

## 5. Anti-Roadmap Rule

The DNA Vault may identify the next required domain, but it may not publish a roadmap of future domains. Each expansion decision must be made from the active constitutional state at the time of decision.

## 6. Creation Boundary

Identifying a next required domain does not create it. Creation requires a separate constitutional action after the signal.
