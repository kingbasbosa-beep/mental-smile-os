# Pre Archive Guardrails V1

Status: ACTIVE_GUARDRAILS
Prompt Asset: PROMPT_ASSET_050

## Rules

No deletion before classification.

No archive movement before verification.

No runtime migration before inventory completion.

No Firebase purification before active runtime declaration.

No YAML purification before asset ownership verification.

## Additional Guardrails

- No unknown workspace root may be used.
- No archive candidate may be moved without future archive migration package.
- No historical evidence may be deleted.
- No active documentation root may be treated as legacy.
- No legacy runtime may be declared active without constitutional rebuild.
- No source asset may enter OS by copy/paste.

## Boundary

These guardrails do not perform archive movement.

They define rules for future archive preparation only.
