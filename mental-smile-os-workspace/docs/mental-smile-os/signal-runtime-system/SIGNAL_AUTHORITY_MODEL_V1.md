# SIGNAL_AUTHORITY_MODEL_V1

Status: REGISTERED

## Authority Rule

Signals are operational evidence and routing packages. They are not final authority unless an approved governance process consumes and validates them.

## Authority Boundaries

| Actor | Signal Authority | Forbidden |
|---|---|---|
| Source Zone | Owns emitted source signal. | Transfer ownership by forwarding. |
| Gateway | Receives, classifies, counts, buffers, forwards. | Govern, approve, execute, mutate foreign zones. |
| Monitoring | Observes and reports. | Approve or execute. |
| Strategic Intelligence | Analyzes summaries and recommends. | Execute or govern. |
| Smart Archive | Stores snapshots and history. | Mutate source-zone runtime. |
| Owner | Approves constitutional changes. | Bypass registry, audit, compliance. |

## Authority Violation

A signal violates authority if it bypasses its local gateway, transfers ownership without doctrine, or causes monitoring/strategic/archive to mutate runtime directly.
