# MASTER_ROUTE_AUTHORITY_MODEL_V1

Status: REGISTERED

## Authority Rule

Route authority belongs to the route owner, but runtime movement must still respect source-zone ownership and target-zone boundaries.

## Authority Matrix

| Actor | May Define | May Approve | May Execute | Forbidden |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | Constitutional route doctrine and approved changes. | Constitutional route evolution. | No direct runtime execution. | Bypass registry, audit, or compliance. |
| Zone Owner | Internal route definition for its zone. | Local route readiness within approved doctrine. | Zone-local runtime only after implementation approval. | Mutate foreign zones. |
| Gateway | Receive, classify, count, summarize, buffer, forward. | No. | No business or governance execution. | Govern, authorize, own foreign signals. |
| Monitoring | Observe and escalate. | No. | No. | Approve, mutate, execute. |
| Strategic Intelligence | Analyze summaries and recommend. | No. | No. | Execute, mutate, govern. |
| Smart Archive | Store, snapshot, retrieve, preserve. | No. | No runtime execution. | Mutate source zones. |

## Owner Constraint

Owner authority is constitutional authority, not bypass authority. Owner route changes must pass registry, audit, and compliance.
