## Freeze Meaning

Archive freeze means:

- Historical eras are formally identified.
- Their meaning is locked.
- Their lessons are preserved.
- Their educational signals become reusable.
- Their files remain physically untouched.
- No old era can re-enter active runtime identity without Owner decision.

Archive freeze does not mean:

- Deleting files.
- Moving folders.
- Flattening history.
- Removing prompt memory.
- Hiding inconvenient evidence.
