Reduce navigation noise without deleting historical evidence.

## Reduction Without Deletion

| Strategy | Effect | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Archive cards | Compress era meaning into readable cards. | LOW |
| Knowledge registries | Convert repeated reports into reusable lessons. | LOW |
| Scope registry | Separates active, historical, educational, and deletion candidate zones. | LOW |
| Index-first navigation | Users read registries before raw reports. | LOW |
| Freeze labels | Prevents old eras from reappearing as active. | LOW |

## Estimated Reduction

Visible navigation reduction: 35% to 55%.

Physical file reduction: 0% now.

Future physical reduction after Owner-approved deletion: likely 1% to 5%, because most files are evidence, not trash.

## No-Deletion Reduction Model

1. Keep all files in place.
2. Add archive cards and registries.
3. Treat raw reports as supporting evidence.
4. Use archive cards as primary navigation.
5. Use lessons registry as active engineering knowledge.

## Future Reduction Model

Only after Owner approval:

1. Identify duplicate reports.
2. Confirm final report coverage.
3. Extract lesson.
4. Preserve archive card.
5. Delete only if no unique evidence remains.

## Strategy Verdict

Use semantic reduction now.

Do not use physical deletion now.
