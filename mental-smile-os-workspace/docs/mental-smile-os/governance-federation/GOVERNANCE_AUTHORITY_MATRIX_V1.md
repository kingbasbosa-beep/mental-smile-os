## Escalation Rules

Monitoring may escalate to Owner and Strategic Intelligence.

Strategic Intelligence may recommend to Owner.

Smart Archive may report continuity or integrity gaps to Owner and Monitoring.

## Approval Rules

Only Owner Zone may approve constitutional changes.

Approval must not bypass registries, audit, or constitutional lifecycle.

## Isolation Rules

Owner is not Monitoring.

Monitoring is not Strategic.

Strategic is not Archive.

Archive is not Owner.

No zone may absorb another zone authority.
