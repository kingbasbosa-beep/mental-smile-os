## Authority Separation

| Zone | May | May Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OWNER_ZONE | Approve constitutional changes, federation evolution, legal authority, strategic approval, infrastructure direction, emergency authority. | Bypass registries, audit, or constitutional lifecycle. |
| MONITORING_ZONE | Observe, report, escalate. | Authorize, execute, govern, mutate runtime. |
| STRATEGIC_INTELLIGENCE_ZONE | Analyze, recommend, create strategic reports. | Govern, execute, authorize, mutate runtime. |
| SMART_ARCHIVE_ZONE | Store, retrieve, snapshot, preserve clone packages and history. | Operate, govern, mutate source zones. |

## Rule

No governance zone may absorb another governance zone authority.
