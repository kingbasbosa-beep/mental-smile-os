# Archive Retention Policy V1

Status: RETENTION_POLICY_CREATED
Prompt Asset: PROMPT_ASSET_051

## Purpose

Define retention categories for future archive migration.

## Retention Categories

| Retention Status | Meaning | Deletion Allowed Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permanent Preservation | Must be preserved indefinitely. | NO |
| Long-Term Preservation | Preserve through future generations unless replaced by policy. | NO |
| Optional Preservation | Preserve until future review. | NO |
| Future Deletion Candidate | May be deleted only after future explicit authority. | NO |

## Retention Rules

- Nothing may be deleted before archive classification.
- Historical evidence defaults to long-term preservation.
- Museum artifacts default to permanent preservation.
- Unknown artifacts default to no deletion.
- Future deletion candidates still require explicit future deletion authority.

## Boundary

This policy does not delete anything.
