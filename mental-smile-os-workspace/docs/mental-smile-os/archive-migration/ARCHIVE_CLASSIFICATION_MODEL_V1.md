# Archive Classification Model V1

Status: ACTIVE_CLASSIFICATION_MODEL
Prompt Asset: PROMPT_ASSET_051

## Classifications

| Classification | Definition | Allowed Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACTIVE | Active Mental Smile OS workspace or city package. | Use as active reference. |
| ACTIVE_REFERENCE | Active supporting snapshot, clone, rebirth, or declaration package. | Reference only. |
| ARCHIVE_CANDIDATE | May become archive after lineage verification. | Classify only. |
| HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE | Preserved to prove origin, evolution, or replacement. | Preserve. |
| MUSEUM_ARTIFACT | Important historical milestone or era artifact. | Preserve for museum registry. |
| UNKNOWN | Not yet trusted or classified. | Do not use, move, delete, or archive. |

## Required Fields

Every future archive classification record must include:

- Item ID.
- Item name.
- Source path or reference.
- Classification.
- Origin.
- Replacement or successor.
- Archive status.
- Retention status.
- Risk notes.

## Rule

Nothing may be deleted before archive classification.
