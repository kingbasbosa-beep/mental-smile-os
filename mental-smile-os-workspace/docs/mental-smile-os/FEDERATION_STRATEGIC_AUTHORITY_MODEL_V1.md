# Federation Strategic Authority Model V1

Status: ACTIVE_STRATEGIC_AUTHORITY_MODEL
Prompt Asset: PROMPT_ASSET_033

## Strategic Planning May

- Read Summaries.
- Read Reports.
- Generate Analysis.
- Generate Recommendations.

## Strategic Planning May Not

- Mutate Runtime.
- Execute Operations.
- Authorize Changes.
- Bypass Owner Authority.
- Bypass Registries.

## AI Analysis Boundary

Strategic Planning may later run AI analysis on received summaries and details after separate authorization.

Zone Gateways do not perform AI analysis.
