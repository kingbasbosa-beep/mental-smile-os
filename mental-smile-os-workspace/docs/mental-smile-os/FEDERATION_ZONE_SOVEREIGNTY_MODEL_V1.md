## Zone Sovereignty Doctrine

Each Zone owns:

- Internal Runtime.
- Internal Signals.
- Internal Reports.
- Internal Cards.
- Internal Registries.
- Internal Memory.

Other Zones may not mutate these assets directly.

## Zone Failure Principle

Zone Failure does not equal Federation Failure.

If a zone goes offline:

- Its gateway may remain online.
- Its incoming packages may buffer.
- Other zones continue operating constitutionally.
- Recovery remains zone-local unless Owner-approved federation intervention is required later.

## Sovereignty Rule

Sovereignty protects zone boundaries. It does not authorize isolation from audit, registry lineage, or constitutional memory.
