# Federation Signal Authority Model V1

Status: ACTIVE_SIGNAL_AUTHORITY_MODEL
Prompt Asset: PROMPT_ASSET_033

## Signal Ownership

Signals belong to the Source Zone, not the Destination Zone.

Signal ownership remains attached to origin.

Forwarding does not transfer ownership.

## Signal Authority

| Authority Question | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Who owns the signal? | Source Zone. |
| Who may package it? | Local Zone Gateway. |
| Who may forward it? | Local Zone Gateway through approved Signal Pipe. |
| Who may receive it? | Target Zone Gateway. |
| Who may archive it? | Smart Archive through approved detail/report package. |
| Who may analyze it? | Strategic Planning after receiving summaries/reports. |

## Forbidden

- Destination Zone claiming ownership of source signals.
- Direct cross-zone signal mutation.
- Signal forwarding as authorization.
- Signal packages acting as governance decisions.
