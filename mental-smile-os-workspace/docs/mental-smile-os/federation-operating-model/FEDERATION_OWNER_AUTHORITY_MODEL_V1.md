# FEDERATION_OWNER_AUTHORITY_MODEL_V1

## Purpose

Define Owner Authority as the top constitutional approval layer.

## Owner May

- Create doctrine.
- Approve constitutional changes.
- Approve registry changes.
- Approve federation evolution.
- Approve emergency intervention.
- Direct infrastructure evolution.

## Owner May Not

- Bypass registries.
- Bypass audit.
- Bypass lineage.
- Bypass constitutional lifecycle.
- Convert emergency authority into untracked runtime mutation.

## Authority Principle

Owner authority is highest constitutional authority, not a license for hidden bypasses.
