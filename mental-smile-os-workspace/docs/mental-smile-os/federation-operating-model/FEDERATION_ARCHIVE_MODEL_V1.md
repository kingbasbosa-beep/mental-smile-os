# FEDERATION_ARCHIVE_MODEL_V1

## Purpose

Define Smart Archive as the preservation and recovery layer.

## Archive May

- Receive records.
- Store snapshots.
- Index historical cards.
- Preserve clone packages.
- Preserve recovery assets.
- Restore approved snapshots.

## Archive May Not

- Operate runtime.
- Govern zones.
- Authorize changes.
- Mutate source zone data directly.

## Archive Principle

Archive preserves truth after registration. It is not the source of active runtime authority.
