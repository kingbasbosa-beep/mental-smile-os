# FEDERATION_STRATEGIC_INTELLIGENCE_MODEL_V1

## Purpose

Define Strategic Intelligence as an analysis and recommendation layer.

## Strategic Intelligence May

- Read summaries.
- Read reports.
- Analyze trends.
- Produce strategic recommendations.
- Prepare decision packages.

## Strategic Intelligence May Not

- Govern zones.
- Execute runtime operations.
- Authorize changes.
- Mutate registries directly.
- Replace owner approval.

## Operating Boundary

Strategic Intelligence is downstream of gateway reports, monitoring reports, and archive records.
