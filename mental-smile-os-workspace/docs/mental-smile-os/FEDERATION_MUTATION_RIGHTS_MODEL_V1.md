## Mutation Rights

| Mutation Area | Constitutional Authority | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zone Internal Mutation Rights | Owning Zone | Internal only; may not mutate foreign zone assets. |
| Registry Mutation Rights | Owning Registry plus Owner-approved lifecycle | Must preserve lineage and audit. |
| Guide Mutation Rights | Guide authority plus Owner-approved constitutional lifecycle | Must register replacement, alignment, or supersession. |
| Card Mutation Rights | Card registry authority plus related guide lineage | Orphan cards forbidden. |
| Signal Definition Mutation Rights | Signal registry authority plus source-zone lineage | Forwarding never transfers signal ownership. |

## Rule

Mutation authority is constitutional, not technical. No writes, rules, schemas, or backend behavior are created.
