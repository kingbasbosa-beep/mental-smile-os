# Center No Dead Placeholder Rule V1

Status: ACTIVE_NO_DEAD_PLACEHOLDER_RULE
Prompt Asset: PROMPT_ASSET_042

## Rule

No unused placeholder may remain active after any modification.

No deprecated registration field may remain active.

No deprecated public profile field may remain active.

No duplicate field path may remain.

No temporary field may remain without expiry.

## Required Actions

- REMOVE_IMMEDIATELY.
- REPLACE_WITH_NEW_FIELD.
- ARCHIVE_AS_SOURCE_EVIDENCE.
- NEVER_KEEP_IN_ACTIVE_ZONE.
