## 1. Purpose

APP_ROOM_ZONE_GATEWAY is the first constitutional gateway inside Mental Smile OS.

It exists to collect, observe, count, classify, summarize, and forward signal packages.

It must never govern, authorize, or execute.

## 2. Mission

- Receive signal packages.
- Count signal packages.
- Classify signal packages.
- Maintain signal statistics.
- Create human summary.
- Create structured detail report.
- Forward approved signal packages.
- Maintain emergency buffer.
- Replay buffered packages.

## 3. Forbidden Responsibilities

- No Governance.
- No Authorization.
- No Content Decisions.
- No Recommendation Logic.
- No Tool Execution.
- No User Ownership.
- No Business Decisions.
- No AI Decisions.

## 4. Boundary

Documentation only. No runtime, Firebase, signal execution, queue implementation, or AI analysis.
