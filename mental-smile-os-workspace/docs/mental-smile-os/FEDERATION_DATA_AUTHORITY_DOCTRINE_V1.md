## 1. Mission

Define who owns, reads, writes, authorizes, observes, archives, and analyzes federation data across zones, gateways, signals, cards, registries, archive, Strategic Planning, and Owner Authority.

This doctrine is the parent authority for future Firestore rules, backend rules, signal permissions, registry permissions, data ownership policies, and audit boundaries.

## 2. Federation Principle

Zone Failure does not equal Federation Failure.

Each Zone must remain:

- Sovereign.
- Replaceable.
- Independently recoverable.

## 3. Authority Layers

| Authority Layer | May Do | May Not Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zone | Own internal runtime, signals, reports, cards, registries, and memory. | Mutate another zone directly. |
| Gateway | Receive, classify, count, summarize, forward, and buffer. | Govern, authorize, execute, or mutate foreign zone data. |
| Archive | Receive, store, index, snapshot, and restore. | Govern, authorize, or mutate runtime. |
| Strategic Planning | Read summaries/reports and generate analysis/recommendations. | Execute operations, mutate runtime, or authorize changes. |
| Owner | Create doctrine and approve constitutional evolution. | Bypass registries, audit, or constitutional lifecycle. |

## 4. Boundary

Documentation only. No Firebase implementation, Firestore rules, backend code, or schemas are created.

## 5. Federation Continuity Capsule Principle

Each Zone Gateway owns one Continuity Capsule.

Continuity Capsules preserve signal packages temporarily during short-lived zone outages.

Zone Failure does not equal Federation Failure.

Federation Continues During Zone Replacement.

Continuity Capsules may not govern, authorize, analyze, report, own registries, or store long-term data.

## 6. Library Web Zone Authority

LIBRARY_WEB_ZONE owns its districts, content cards, tool cards, research cards, resource cards, internal signals, and internal registries.

Library content recommendations do not belong to App Zone.

Signals may travel through gateways and pipes.

Ownership may not travel with signals.

## 7. Library Web Zone Ready Package

LIBRARY_WEB_ZONE is federation compatible and READY.

Its ownership, districts, gateway, content doctrines, internal network, and boundary models are closed by LIBRARY_WEB_ZONE_PACKAGE_MASTER_CARD_V1.

## 8. Provider Web Federation Split Authority

PROVIDER_REGISTRATION_WEB_ZONE owns provider onboarding, registration, credential submission, document collection, profile draft creation, review preparation, and status tracking.

PROVIDER_PUBLIC_WEB_ZONE owns approved provider public profiles, public discovery, service visibility, contact pathways, trust display, and public resources.

PROVIDER_PUBLIC_WEB_ZONE may not read private registration documents or mutate provider registration.

PROVIDER_REGISTRATION_WEB_ZONE may not control public ranking or publish public profiles directly.

All transfer must pass through gateways and approved card packages.

## 9. Provider Field Parity Authority

Provider public display fields must map to declared provider registration source fields.

Provider Registration fields must declare display, review, internal status, document, or not-displayed status.

No dead placeholder, deprecated active field, duplicate field path, or temporary field without expiry may remain active in provider registration or provider public zones.

## 10. Center Web Federation Split Authority

CENTER_REGISTRATION_WEB_ZONE owns center onboarding, registration, document submission, service declaration, partnership requests, contract preparation, profile draft creation, and status tracking.

CENTER_PUBLIC_WEB_ZONE owns approved center public profiles, service visibility, trust visibility, contact pathways, and public resources.

CENTER_PUBLIC_WEB_ZONE may not access private registration documents or mutate registration data.

CENTER_REGISTRATION_WEB_ZONE may not directly publish public profiles.

All transfer requires approved card packages and gateway routing.

## 11. Commercial Web Zones Ready Authority

Provider Registration, Provider Public, Center Registration, and Center Public zones are READY, independent, and federation compatible.

Field parity and no-dead-placeholder rules are mandatory for Provider and Center commercial public display flows.

## 12. Governance Federation Authority

OWNER_ZONE owns constitutional approval and federation evolution authority.

MONITORING_ZONE observes, reports, and escalates but may not authorize or execute.

STRATEGIC_INTELLIGENCE_ZONE analyzes and recommends but may not govern or execute.

SMART_ARCHIVE_ZONE stores and retrieves but may not operate or mutate source zones.

No governance zone may absorb another governance zone authority.
