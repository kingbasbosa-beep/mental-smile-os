# Discovery Experience Report V1

Result: DISCOVERY_EXPERIENCE_REVIEW_COMPLETE

## Discovery Reality

Mental Smile OS is built around discovery, and the runtime contains the right categories: library, specialists, centers, support modules, provider registration, center registration, client room/dashboard, and early signal concepts.

The gap is execution depth. Discovery exists as routes and surfaces, but it is not yet a fully guided journey.

## Discovery Strengths

- Discovery is not limited to one path; it includes knowledge, providers, centers, family, recovery, and accessibility.
- The library makes safety boundaries visible.
- Support signals give a structured way to ask for help without pretending to diagnose.
- Provider/center capability signals can eventually support meaningful filtering.

## Discovery Gaps

- No clear "start here" decision tree.
- Tool discovery is mostly inactive.
- Provider/center discovery needs visible trust states.
- Library discovery needs clearer categories for first-time humans, not only internal signal categories.
- Accessibility discovery needs visible filters and entry points.
- Recovery and family journeys need next-step continuity after the first click.

## Discovery Verdict

Discovery architecture exists. Human discovery experience is partial.

Recommended status: NOT READY FOR FULL PUBLIC DISCOVERY LAUNCH.
