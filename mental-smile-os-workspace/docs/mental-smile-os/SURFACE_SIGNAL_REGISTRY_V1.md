# Surface Signal Registry V1

Status: ACTIVE_SURFACE_SIGNAL_REGISTRY
Prompt Asset: PROMPT_ASSET_014
Topology Source: PROMPT_ASSET_013

## 1. Purpose

Surface Signal Registry defines signals used to register and validate approved Mental Smile OS surfaces.

These signals do not create runtime, permissions, Firebase rules, or new surfaces.

## 3. Signal Boundary

Surface signals may describe state and readiness.

Surface signals may not authorize runtime construction by themselves.
