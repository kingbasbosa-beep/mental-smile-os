# City Overview

Mental Smile City Generation 1 is organized into four simple layers.

## Residential Layer

The Residential Layer is the app-side experience. It includes the first user-facing journey:

- Splash.
- Home.
- Client Registration.
- Client Room.
- Provider Room.
- Center Room.

This layer is where people enter, choose tools, receive signals, and move through personal or professional rooms.

## Knowledge Layer

The Knowledge Layer is the Library Web Zone.

It contains learning, tools, research, accessibility, recovery, family support, and resources. It is not part of the app room. It is its own zone with its own gateway and internal districts.

## Commercial Layer

The Commercial Layer separates registration from public display:

- Provider Registration Zone.
- Provider Public Zone.
- Center Registration Zone.
- Center Public Zone.

This keeps private onboarding separate from public discovery.

## Governance Layer

The Governance Layer protects the city:

- Owner Zone.
- Monitoring Zone.
- Strategic Intelligence Zone.
- Smart Archive Zone.

It observes, audits, archives, and strategizes. It does not operate the user experience.

## High-Level Topology

App Zone -> App Gateway -> Signal Pipes -> Other Zone Gateways

Library Zone -> Library Gateway -> Signal Pipes -> Federation

Commercial Zones -> Their Gateways -> Approved Card Packages

Governance Zones -> Observe, analyze, archive, and approve through separated authority
