# Constitutional Doctrine

Mental Smile OS uses constitutional rules so the platform can grow cleanly.

## No Legacy

Old material can be used as source evidence, but it cannot be copied directly into the new OS.

The rule is:

Extract -> Purify -> Reclassify -> Rebuild -> Register

## No Dead Placeholders

No unused field, route, asset path, rule, or placeholder should remain active just because it existed before.

Anything active must have a purpose, owner, status, and lifecycle.

## No Orphan Cards

Cards describe screens, assets, tools, signals, zones, and packages.

No card may exist without lineage, ownership, and registry placement.

## Zone Sovereignty

Each zone owns its internal work.

Other zones may communicate with it, but they may not mutate it directly.

## Federation Doctrine

The city is a federation, not one giant app.

If one zone is unavailable, the whole city should not fail. Gateways and continuity capsules preserve short-term signal flow and allow replacement.

## Signal Doctrine

Signals replaced direct dependencies.

Instead of one room talking directly to another zone, signals travel through gateways and approved pipes. This keeps the architecture understandable, recoverable, and safer to expand.
