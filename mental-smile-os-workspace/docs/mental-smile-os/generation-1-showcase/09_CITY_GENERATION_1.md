# City Generation 1

Generation 1 is the first complete constitutional snapshot of Mental Smile City.

It captures what exists, what is stable, and what can be cloned.

## Completed

Generation 1 includes:

- App Zone screen packages.
- Client Room doctrine and asset binding.
- Provider and Center room definitions.
- Library Web Zone.
- Commercial Web Zones.
- Governance Federation Layer.
- Zone Gateway doctrine.
- Signal Pipe doctrine.
- Federation Authority doctrine.
- Continuity Capsule doctrine.
- City purity rules.
- Pure Firebase rules doctrine package.
- Pure YAML doctrine package.

## Stable

The city now has stable boundaries for:

- Zones.
- Gateways.
- Signals.
- Authority.
- Memory.
- Clone packages.
- Public presentation.
- Future implementation purity.

## Cloneable

The city is designed to produce future zones and gateways from registered packages instead of improvising new structures.

Generation 1 is not the final product.

It is the first clean city foundation.
