# Next Generation

The next generation of Mental Smile OS can build on Generation 1 without collapsing the architecture.

## Tools

Tool systems can be developed as registered cards, packages, and discovery relationships.

Tools should remain user-friendly and signal-driven, not profile-heavy.

## AI

AI can support research, summaries, accessibility, and strategic analysis.

It should stay inside clear zones and never bypass authority, ownership, or audit boundaries.

## Research

Research can grow inside the Library Web Zone and serve providers, centers, and strategic intelligence through approved signal routes.

## Accessibility

Accessibility can become a first-class district, asset family, and future app/web support layer.

## External Apps Federation

Future apps can connect as federation members instead of being merged into the core platform.

## Partner Federation

Partners can receive separated onboarding, public presentation, gateway routing, and authority boundaries.

## Future Expansion

Future expansion should follow the same principle:

Do not copy the past.

Extract, purify, reclassify, rebuild, and register.
