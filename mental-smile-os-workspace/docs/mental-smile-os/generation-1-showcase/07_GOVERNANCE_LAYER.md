# Governance Layer

The Governance Layer protects Mental Smile OS without operating the platform directly.

It is separate from the App Zone, Library Zone, and Commercial Zones.

## Owner Zone

The Owner Zone holds constitutional approval and federation evolution authority.

It protects direction, doctrine, and major platform decisions.

## Monitoring Zone

The Monitoring Zone observes:

- Signal health.
- Gateway health.
- Continuity status.
- Escalation visibility.

It may observe and report. It may not authorize or execute.

## Strategic Intelligence Zone

The Strategic Intelligence Zone analyzes summaries and trends.

It may recommend. It may not govern or execute.

## Smart Archive Zone

The Smart Archive Zone stores:

- Snapshots.
- Clone packages.
- Historical reports.
- Recovery assets.
- Continuity assets.

It stores and retrieves. It does not operate source zones.

## Authority Separation

Owner is not Monitoring.

Monitoring is not Strategic Intelligence.

Strategic Intelligence is not Archive.

Archive is not Owner.

No governance zone may absorb another.
