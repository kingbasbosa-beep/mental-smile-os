# App Zone

The App Zone is the primary mobile and app-side experience.

It is not a dashboard maze. It is a sequence of meaningful spaces.

## Splash

Splash introduces Mental Smile with a calm Arabic-first entry.

It avoids clinical promises, booking language, and legacy visual clutter.

## Login

Login is the clean identity entry screen between Splash and Home.

It contains email or phone, password, login action, language toggle, create account link, and forgot password link.

It does not collect profile data, tool choices, recommendations, or clinical labels.

## Home

Home is the first navigation gateway of the app experience.

It links users toward app rooms and approved external zones without becoming one giant menu.

## Registration

Client Registration creates identity only.

It does not collect interests, recommendations, profile data, tool preferences, avatar choices, or clinical labels.

## Client Room

The Client Room is a personal space.

It contains:

- Tool Board.
- Notification Panel.
- Picture Frame.
- Motivation Note.
- Exit Cup.
- Left and right switchers.

Users choose tools. Tools drive discovery signals. The room does not perform matching or diagnosis.

## Provider Room

The Provider Room is a professional workspace for clinicians and specialists.

It includes a tool board, notification panel, psychology-scope assistant surface, research desk, public profile shortcut, and exit action.

## Center Room

The Center Room is a professional workspace for centers and organizations.

It supports operational focus while staying separated from registration and public web zones.

## Exit Social Links

Room exit may lead to a calm public presence screen instead of sending users only to Login.

The screen may show official public Mental Smile links such as website, email, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Telegram, X, Zoom, GitHub, and domain/web presence.

It may not show private account data, admin tools, internal docs, Firebase links, or credentials.

## App Gateway

All App Zone signals terminate at the App Room Zone Gateway before leaving the zone.

No app screen talks directly to another zone.
