## Owner May

- Create Doctrine.
- Approve Constitutional Changes.
- Approve Registry Changes.
- Approve Federation Evolution.

## Owner May Not

- Bypass Registries.
- Bypass Audit.
- Bypass Constitutional Lifecycle.

## Owner Authority Rule

Owner Authority is supreme in constitutional approval, but it remains bound to lineage, registry, audit, and lifecycle rules.
