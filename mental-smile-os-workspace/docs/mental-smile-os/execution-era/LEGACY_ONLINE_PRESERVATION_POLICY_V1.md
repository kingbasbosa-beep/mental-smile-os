# Legacy Online Preservation Policy V1

Status: LEGACY_ONLINE_PRESERVATION_POLICY_CREATED
Prompt Asset: PROMPT_ASSET_063

## Purpose

Define how old Firebase projects and old GitHub repositories may remain online as ownership lineage and historical evidence.

## Policy

Old Firebase projects and old GitHub repositories may remain online as:

- HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE.
- SOURCE_REPOSITORY.
- ARCHIVE_REFERENCE.

## Rules

They must not be used as active runtime.

They must not be deployed from.

They must not be evolved.

They must not be treated as Generation 1 authority.

They must not bypass the Generation 1 execution backlog.

## Preservation Reason

Legacy online history proves ownership, lineage, decision history, evolution, and migration context.

Preservation does not equal active authority.
